Meet them

  • Meet Connie — Her story, her file and the whole family.
  • The Family file — Likes, dislikes, opinions and secrets for everyone in the house, with a time machine to watch them grow up.
  • Family photo album — Thirty-one years of awkward family photos. Somebody always blinks. Turn them over to read the back.
  • The Squabbles — Petty fights and long cold wars, each about a real thing that used to go wrong with a PC. Pick a side.
  • Trading Cards — Every Ventional on a card. Connie is the holofoil.
  • Sticker sheet — Print stickers for your laptop.
  • The Sunday Funnies — Strips and gags about Connie and her family, plus a fresh strip made from the timeline every time.

Dress up and earn

  • Connie’s closet — Pick her look, accessory and color.
  • Prize counter — Trade game tickets for stickers, colors, looks and accessories.
  • Memory Manager — Fit the game into 640K by editing CONFIG.SYS. Earn stars, unlock looks.

Take her home

  • The Mall (under construction) — Stickers, cards, pins, plush and custom iPods. A mock-up for now.
