Play

Games, things to make, and things to watch.

Open Play in the interactive museum

Every day

  • Daily Dig — A new puzzle every morning.
  • Retro Bingo — Cross off the squares.

Games

  • All the games — Trivia, Higher or Lower, Timeline Sort, Mystery Photo.
  • Higher or Lower — Which launch price was bigger?
  • Timeline Sort — Put five finds in order, oldest first.
  • Mystery Photo — Name the blurry photo before it comes into focus.
  • Disk Error Hangman — Guess the machine.
  • Rig challenge — Pick a year, spend your points, and run the era’s real games.
  • The Basement — Staff only. Find your way out.

Make something

  • Ad Lab — Make a retro tribute ad for any item, with stickers, and download it.

Watch and listen

  • Retro Theater — A little CRT TV for videos.
  • Jukebox — Chiptunes through four kinds of sound card.

Show-off screens

  • Demo kiosk — Attract mode for a spare screen, like a demo station in a toy-store aisle. Scenes and slides are made fresh from the whole museum.
  • Animation library — Every move Connie and her family can make, and a scene composer for any timeline entry.
