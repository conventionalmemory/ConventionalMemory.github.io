Which board do I have?

Boards changed during production. These notes come from the linked sources, and many are only partly documented, so check the board in front of you.

  • CoCo 1 F board (Medium confidence). How to tell: Called out as an F board; CoCoPedia lists D, E, ET and F boards for the CoCo 1. Capacitors: A PDF by David Kroeker maps capacitors from C9 to C84, 1uF to 10000uF, 16V to 50V. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • CoCo 1 D and E boards (Medium confidence). How to tell: D boards are 26-3001, 26-3002 and 26-3003; E boards are 26-3002A and 26-3003A. Capacitors: No cap list for these found. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • CoCo 2 USA made (board 8709416) (Medium confidence). How to tell: 26-3026, 3026A, 3026B and 3027 with upright RF modulator and large TO-3 pass transistor. Capacitors: No cap list found. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • CoCo 2 Korean made (boards 20261043, 20261044, 20261058) (Medium confidence). How to tell: 26-3134 and 3136 use 20261043, the A versions 20261044, B versions 20261058; flat modulator, TO-220 pass transistor, shielded transformer. Capacitors: No cap list found. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
