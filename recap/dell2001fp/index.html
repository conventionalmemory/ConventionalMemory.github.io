Recap the Dell UltraSharp 2001FP monitor

Which board do I have?

Boards changed during production. These notes come from the linked sources, and many are only partly documented, so check the board in front of you.

  • 2001FP backlight inverter AMBIT T05I031.00 (Low confidence). How to tell: Board marking read from one owner's post. Capacitors: A forum poster found burn spots on this inverter board and fixed the burning smell by replacing the inverter; no capacitor values. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source

Where to start

Clues from write-ups and forum threads, not a diagnosis. Low confidence means one thread or one repair; high means several write-ups agree.

  • Burning smell (Low confidence). One 2001FP with a burning smell was suspected of bad caps, but the AMBIT inverter was the real fault, so check the inverter first. Source

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Tip: Check the external supply before the inverter (Medium confidence). A 2001FP that worked about three minutes then blacked out with a burning smell had burn marks on the inverter board, and a used AMBIT T05I031.00 replacement cost about $18 and fixed it. A reply first advised measuring the output of the external power supply to rule it out. Source

Before you start

  • Boards changed during production. Check your board number against the guides before you order parts.
  • A tube and its high-voltage circuit can hold a lethal charge. Do not open the tube, and leave the analog board to a professional.
