Recap the Macintosh Quadra 700 and 900

Which board do I have?

Boards changed during production. These notes come from the linked sources, and many are only partly documented, so check the board in front of you.

  • Quadra 700 supply, Astec AA15831 (Medium confidence). How to tell: Astec AA15831. Capacitors: Main board 9 caps 4.7uF to 2200uF, daughterboard 2 caps (2.2uF and 4.7uF), AC input board 4 caps including film safety; a discontinued 180uF at C25 can become 220uF. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source

Where to start

Clues from write-ups and forum threads, not a diagnosis. Low confidence means one thread or one repair; high means several write-ups agree.

  • Astec supply recap (Medium confidence). A Quadra 700 guide lists the caps in the Astec AA15831 supply (main, daughter and AC input boards) with a Digi-Key cart and notes on obsolete values and substitutions. Source

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Tip: C25 180uF is obsolete (Medium confidence). In the Quadra 700 Astec AA15831 recap list the post says the original 180uF cap C25 is largely obsolete so a 220uF part should be chosen instead, and that film safety caps may also need substitution. Voltages run from 10V to 400V across the main, daughter and AC input boards. Source

Before you start

  • Boards changed during production. Check your board number against the guides before you order parts.
  • A mains power supply can hold a dangerous charge. If you are not trained to work on one, have a professional do it.
