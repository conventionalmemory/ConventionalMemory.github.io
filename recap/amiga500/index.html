Which board do I have?

Boards changed during production. These notes come from the linked sources, and many are only partly documented, so check the board in front of you.

  • A500 rev 3 (Medium confidence). How to tell: No silkscreen above the RAM and all 16 RAM positions filled; B-52 Rock Lobster etched in copper. Capacitors: A UK shop says its A500 recap service covers every revision; no per-revision cap split found. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • A500 rev 5 (Medium confidence). How to tell: White A500 silkscreen above 16 RAM chips and vias behind expansion pins 1, 3 and 5. Capacitors: No per-revision cap split found. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • A500 rev 6A (Medium confidence). How to tell: White A500 silkscreen above only 8 RAM positions and JPL marked between expansion pins 7 and 9. Capacitors: No per-revision cap split found. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • A500 Plus rev 8A (Medium confidence). How to tell: Silkscreen reads A500+ and E702, E703 and E704 are marked near expansion pins 7, 9 and 13. Capacitors: Carries a battery on the board that can leak; the shop recap service covers it with the other revisions. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source

Where to start

Clues from write-ups and forum threads, not a diagnosis. Low confidence means one thread or one repair; high means several write-ups agree.

  • Battery first (Low confidence). A mailing list post says electrolytics are not the headline failure on the A500, and the trapdoor battery is. Source
  • Recap videos and services (Low confidence). One restoration video covers cleaning and capacitor replacement, and a recapping service replaces all electrolytics on the A500 and A500+. Neither gives a list. Source

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Tip: A500 caps are through-hole (Medium confidence). An A1200 write-up contrasts the A500, with through-hole electrolytics, against the A600 and A1200, whose electrolytics are mostly surface-mount and especially unreliable. So an A500 recap is the easier job, and its battery is the bigger worry. Source
