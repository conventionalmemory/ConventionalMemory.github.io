Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Story: T1900 shuts off after one second (Medium confidence). A Toshiba T1900 that ran for a second then shut off had a PSU cap (6.3V 1000uF, the smaller brown one) reading 69 ohm ESR. Swapping to a 10V 1000uF fixed it, and the author says the cap looked fine and recommends an ESR meter. Source
  • Story: T1200: leaks in PSU and inverter (Low confidence). A Russian restoration of a T1200 found leaked electrolyte in the power supply board and display inverter but no board damage. The author swapped the BIOS battery for a CR2032 behind a diode and fitted 18650 cells in 2s2p with a BMS. Source
  • Tip: Libretto RTC battery corrosion (Low confidence). A poster repairing Librettos found the clock battery had corroded vias on a 70CT, fixed with 0.1mm wire, and used an external CR2032 pack behind diodes at about 2.8V. He also noted keyboard traces on a 50CT corrode, with test pads breaking first. Source
  • Story: T1000LE: caps, SRAM battery and a sneaky PSU (Low confidence). An EEVblog episode lists bad caps, a backup battery SRAM problem and a sneaky PSU fault on a T1000LE, using thermal imaging and microscopy, and ends with the repair listed as busted. Page text is only the episode summary. Source
