Recap the Apple Lisa

The Lisa power supply and video board use low-ESR electrolytics (2200 uF 16V HF-type) that people routinely replace.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each capacitor as you replace it. It saves in your browser.

Worth a recap. Reports say it is a common and useful fix on this machine.

Which board do I have?

Boards changed during production. These notes come from the linked sources, and many are only partly documented, so check the board in front of you.

  • Lisa PSU 620-6103 (Medium confidence). How to tell: Part number as asked about by an owner. Capacitors: Six 2200uF 16V caps: four identical and two smaller marked HF. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source

Where to start

Clues from write-ups and forum threads, not a diagnosis. Low confidence means one thread or one repair; high means several write-ups agree.

  • Power supply electrolytics (Low confidence). A forum thread about the 620-6103 supply discusses 2200 uF 16V low-ESR caps and which replacement series to use. It is thin, and we could not open the best Lisa cap list. Source

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Maybe: Reform the Lisa PSU caps instead (Low confidence). In a 620-6103 supply thread the most experienced reply said good original 105C caps can last well beyond 10,000 hours with airflow and may only need reforming, and that any reasonably low-ESR replacement will do. A Nichicon UPJ1C222MHD6 was floated as a replacement but flagged as unverified. Source
  • Tip: ProFile supply has nine RIFA caps (search page snippet) (Low confidence). A TinkerDifferent search results page (not the original threads) shows posters finding nine RIFA caps in a ProFile drive power supply, discussing whether X2 parts can stand in for the original X-rated ones and RIFA versus WIMA for X1 replacements. Treat as a pointer to those threads. Source
  • Story: A failed RIFA also blew a fuse (search page snippet) (Low confidence). The same search page lists a ProFile 5MB restoration where one failed RIFA cap in the drive supply also blew a fuse, with the owner asking for suitable replacements. The drive supply is separate from the Lisa's own PSU, but they share the RIFA risk. Source

Before you start

  • Boards changed during production. Check your board number against the guides before you order parts.
  • A mains power supply can hold a dangerous charge. If you are not trained to work on one, have a professional do it.
  • A tube and its high-voltage circuit can hold a lethal charge. Do not open the tube, and leave the analog board to a professional.
