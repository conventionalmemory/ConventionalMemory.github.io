Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Watch out: 7805 failure can leak input voltage (Medium confidence). A Plus/4 write-up warns the 7805 regulator can fail so its input leaks onto its output and damages the board, and suggests checking 9-12Vac unloaded in and 5.1-5.3Vdc unloaded out. The actual fault was a bad 4164 at U18, found with a diagnostic cartridge, even though all electrolytics were replaced with a Retroleum pack. Source
  • Tip: Check 5V at the bottom pins first (Low confidence). A forum answer on a black-screen Plus/4 says to measure 5V DC across the bottom two power connector pins (the upper pins carry 9V AC), looking for a steady 1 to 1.5A, then inspect for blown caps and burnt resistors. One reported fix combined an EPROM for the 317054-1 ROM, heatsinks and new electrolytics. Source
  • Tip: Original brick is not repairable (Low confidence). A hobbyist page calls the original Plus/4 power supply a non-repairable black brick and suggests a more reliable pack. It also says a power connector from a dead C64 board can be retrofitted so a C64 supply can be used. Source
