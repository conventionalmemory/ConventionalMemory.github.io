Where to start

Clues from write-ups and forum threads, not a diagnosis. Low confidence means one thread or one repair; high means several write-ups agree.

  • Leaking electrolytics (Low confidence). A kit seller's note describes electrolytic failures and leakage, and says its kit is about 13 caps. It is a seller note, not a parts list. Source

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Tip: Only aluminium electrolytics leak; clean corrosion carefully (Low confidence). In a cctech thread on Model 100 and HX-20 cap kits, one poster says he has only seen damage from the aluminium electrolytics and that the kits hold about 13 caps. He suggests diluted hydrochloric acid rather than vinegar on corroded solder, which another poster disputes only on the drilling advice. Source
  • Watch out: Leak corrosion makes solder stop flowing (Low confidence). The thread notes leaked electrolyte can change the solder so it will not melt normally. Posters disagreed on drilling out plated holes (one for, one strongly against, preferring fresh solder and sustained heat), and one favoured SMD replacements to protect the board. Source
