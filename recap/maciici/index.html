Which board do I have?

Boards changed during production. These notes come from the linked sources, and many are only partly documented, so check the board in front of you.

  • IIci supply, Astec (Low confidence). How to tell: Maker named on the supply; a console5 note warns not to trust the 614 or 699 Apple numbers and to check the real maker. Capacitors: No values in the thread. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • IIci supply, GE 9T89Y5005G11 (Apple 699-0392) (Medium confidence). How to tell: GE model number on the supply. Capacitors: A recap kit exists for it; no values in the thread. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source
  • IIsi supply (Sony) (Medium confidence). How to tell: Sony supply of about 1991 in one article. Capacitors: 12 electrolytics: 2 SMD on a daughterboard and 10 through-hole, including a 400V cap in the AC section. Capacitor list differs from other revisions: not known. Source

Where to start

Clues from write-ups and forum threads, not a diagnosis. Low confidence means one thread or one repair; high means several write-ups agree.

  • IIsi power supply (Medium confidence). One write-up counts 12 caps in the IIsi PSU, including two SMD on a daughterboard and a 400V part. The author recapped only 3 of the 12 and defers to a repair service's list for values. Source
  • Leaky IIsi board (Low confidence). A logic board testing thread confirms the IIsi's leaky-cap reputation and the split between PSU and board problems. Source
  • IIci supply (Low confidence). A request thread for the IIci supply points to a wiki list and a Console5 kit for Astec or GE supplies. Source

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Story: Five IIcis, five leak patterns (Medium confidence). A refurbisher reported five IIcis that all showed electrolytic leakage, with lifted pads at C2 and C3 near the sound chips, corroded 74HC132-family logic chips, battery damage on one and contamination on another. He replaced leaked logic chips and advises replacing the 74ALS240 below C16 if NuBus slots fail. Source
  • Story: Shutdown after hours traced to leak damage (Low confidence). A IIci recapped with tantalums still shut down after hours, and the cause was broken traces around UE13 and UD13 plus a failed 74HC132 at UE13, found by continuity testing. Leaked electrolyte had hidden damage beyond what was visible. Source
  • Tip: Alcohol bath after caps are off (Low confidence). One restorer received a IIci with the surface-mount caps already removed but corrosion remaining and a badly leaking PSU. He replaced every cap, cleaned the board in an isopropyl alcohol bath and serviced the floppy drive. Source

Before you start

  • Boards changed during production. Check your board number against the guides before you order parts.
  • A mains power supply can hold a dangerous charge. If you are not trained to work on one, have a professional do it.
