Where to start

Clues from write-ups and forum threads, not a diagnosis. Low confidence means one thread or one repair; high means several write-ups agree.

  • Most cap-prone consoles (Low confidence). An AtariAge thread ranks NEC consoles, Game Gear, pre-1995 CD consoles (Sega CD Model 1, LaserActive, 3DO) and the Xbox clock cap among the worst. It is community opinion. Source

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Story: FM Towns Marty recap exists (Low confidence). Hitman's Museum's front page lists a Fujitsu FM Towns Marty entry that involves a full recap, but the summary there gives no values or symptoms. We found nothing else on the other rare consoles. Source
