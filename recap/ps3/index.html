Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us. Check part references and values against your own board.

  • Watch out: NEC/TOKIN swap is hard and may not fix it (Low confidence). A Shmups thread on replacing the NEC/TOKIN caps (the poster describes eight 1200uF on fat models and four 1000uF on slims) warns of ground planes and stubborn solder that lift pads, height clashes with the RF shield, and says to use shorter parts. One tester who replaced all eight with tantalums reported it did not work. Source
  • Maybe: Matching ESR when replacing (Low confidence). In another Shmups thread, a poster theorised the original NEC Tokins are around 1200uF 2.5V with very low ESR and that several small Panasonic 330uF 2.5V tantalums in parallel per position could match it, while flagging that the mod bridges rails the original left separate. This is speculation, not a proven fix. Source
  • Tip: YLOD is a symptom (Low confidence). On an iFixit YLOD question, one answer said a heat-gun or thermal-paste fix is only temporary and recommended replacing the NEC/TOKIN caps by SMD soldering. Another reminded the asker that YLOD is only a symptom, so diagnose what is actually failing rather than swapping parts blindly. Source
