Replace the Dreamcast clock battery (and the controller port fuse)

The clock battery sits on the controller board and is a rechargeable cell that eventually fails. While the board is out it is a good moment to check the F1 port fuse.

Battery · Careful · about 60 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The Dreamcast asks for the date and time every time you switch it on, or the controller ports go dead.

Before you start

  • Diodes have polarity. Install the diode with the cathode ring facing the way ConsoleMods shows.
  • Do not just swap in a non-rechargeable cell without the diode and resistor change.
  • If you cannot desolder cleanly, stop. The board is easy to damage.

Board revisions and models

The ConsoleMods mod describes the stock ML2032 controller board. Check that your board matches its photos before cutting or desoldering anything.

Step by step

  1. Confirm the symptom Set the clock, power off, unplug for a minute and boot again. If it asks again, the battery is not holding.
  2. Open the console Unplug everything, remove the top cover and the four Phillips screws that hold the board.
  3. Disconnect cables Unplug the controller cable and the fan connector, then lift the board out.
  4. Locate the battery It is an ML2032 rechargeable on the controller board. Note its orientation before removing it.
  5. Remove the old cell Desolder the three legs of the old holder and clean the pads.
  6. Choose your fix Either fit a new ML2032, or follow the ConsoleMods mod that adds a CR2032 holder with a Schottky diode and removes a resistor leg. Do not drop a non-rechargeable cell into the stock charging circuit unmodified.
  7. Check the F1 fuse If controller ports were dead, replace the F1 fuse with a 0.4A self-resetting type while you have the board out.
  8. Reassemble Reconnect the fan and controller cable, refit the board and top cover.
  9. Test across a power cycle Set the date, power off, unplug for several minutes and boot again to confirm it keeps time.

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us.

  • Story (Low confidence). A Dreamcast that lost its clock on every boot had an ML2032 that had failed. The repairer fitted a soldered holder so future swaps are easy. Source
  • Tip (Medium confidence). ConsoleMods says to replace the F1 fuse while accessing the board, and that it can blow if you unplug a controller while the console is on. Source
