Reduce yellowing on a console shell (retrobright)

Retrobrite uses hydrogen peroxide and UV light to lighten yellowed plastic. Vapor methods are described by restorers, but the process needs care.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Case · Careful · about 240 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: Grey or white plastic has turned yellow or uneven.

Before you start

  • Peroxide irritates skin and eyes. Wear gloves and eye protection.
  • UV light can damage eyes. Use protective eyewear.
  • Results can be uneven and may reverse over time.
  • Do not treat parts with labels, paint or electronics.

Step by step

  1. Disassemble the shell Remove all electronics, labels and rubber parts.
  2. Wash the plastic Clean it with soap and water.
  3. Protect what should not be treated Keep stickers, painted parts and metal away.
  4. Choose your method A vapor method places parts above peroxide solution in a UV-lit space.
  5. Set up safely Wear gloves and eye protection and work in a ventilated area.
  6. Expose to UV Treat in short rounds and check often.
  7. Rinse and dry Remove parts and rinse thoroughly.
  8. Protect Apply a UV protectant afterwards.
  9. Reassemble Refit all components.
