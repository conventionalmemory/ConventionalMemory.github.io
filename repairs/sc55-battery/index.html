Check the internal battery in a Roland Sound Canvas

Sound Canvas modules carry a small lithium battery that can leak and damage the board over time. Opening the unit, checking and removing the battery is a short job that can save the board.

Battery · Easy · about 30 minutes. Try it, but check first. This will not fix every machine, so read the sources first.

You are in the right place if: Patch memory resets or settings are not remembered, or you have never checked the internal battery and want to avoid a leak.

Before you start

  • Leak residue irritates skin and eyes; wear gloves.
  • If you find corroded capacitors or traces, stop and see a repair guide.

Board revisions and models

Battery position and type may differ between SC-55, SC-55mkII, SC-88 and SC-88Pro.

Step by step

  1. Unplug the unit Disconnect power and all cables and put the unit on a clean surface.
  2. Open the case Remove the screws and lift the cover. Photograph the layout.
  3. Find the battery Locate the small battery on the board and check around it for crust.
  4. Judge the condition If there is residue, treat it as a leak and clean the area before refitting power.
  5. Remove the battery Take it out of its holder or desolder it, noting polarity.
  6. Clean any residue Dab with a mild acid such as white vinegar, scrub with isopropyl alcohol and a soft brush, then let it dry.
  7. Fit a new cell or holder Fit a matching cell in a holder and reassemble.
  8. Test the unit Power up and play the demo songs to check sound and settings.
