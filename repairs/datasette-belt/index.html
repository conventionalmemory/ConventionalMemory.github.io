Get a Commodore Datasette turning and loading reliably

A tired belt, a dirty pinch roller and a misaligned head are the usual reasons a Datasette stops loading. Start with cleaning and a belt swap before touching head alignment.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Drive · Medium · about 60 minutes. Try it, but check first. This will not fix every machine, so read the sources first.

You are in the right place if: The tape does not turn, the motor runs but the spindle does not, or programs load with errors.

Before you start

  • Keep any magnet or demagnetiser away from your tape collection.
  • Do not over-tighten the azimuth screw; mark its position first.

Datasette variants differ slightly in mechanism.

Step by step

  1. Test the basics Check the cable, the power and the computer's tape port with a known-good tape.
  2. Open the Datasette Remove the screws and lift the cover, photographing the mechanism.
  3. Inspect the belt Look for a loose, cracked or sticky belt.
  4. Replace the belt Fit a replacement belt of the right size, cleaning the pulleys first.
  5. Clean the pinch roller Clean the pinch roller with isopropyl alcohol and replace it if it is hard or shiny.
  6. Clean the heads Wipe the head and tape guides gently with alcohol on a swab.
  7. Demagnetise the head Use a tape head demagnetiser if you have one, keeping it away from tapes.
  8. Check azimuth If tapes still fail, consider the head azimuth adjustment, noting its original position first.
  9. Load a test program Try a known-good tape, then others.
