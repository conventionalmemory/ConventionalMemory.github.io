Replace the GameCube clock battery with a holder

The GameCube clock cell is soldered to the mainboard. Replacing it with a holder makes future changes easy.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Battery · Careful · about 45 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The GameCube asks for the date and time and loses its settings each time it is unplugged.

Before you start

  • Do not overheat the mainboard pads.
  • Remove the old cell before heating the pads for a long time.

Step by step

  1. Confirm the symptom Set the clock, unplug the console for a while and check if the settings vanished.
  2. Open the console Unplug and open the case with the correct security driver.
  3. Locate the battery It is a soldered CR2032 on the mainboard.
  4. Measure it One restorer found the original at under 0.8V.
  5. Remove the old cell Desolder the tabs carefully and clean the pads.
  6. Fit a holder Solder in a CR2032 holder and insert a new cell.
  7. Reassemble and test Set the date and confirm it survives a power cycle.
