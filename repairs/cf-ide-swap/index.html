Replace a failing IDE hard drive with a CF or SD adapter

A CompactFlash or SD-to-IDE adapter lets a modern card replace a failing IDE disk in many older machines. It is silent, cool and usually a drop-in swap.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Drive · Easy · about 45 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The old IDE drive grinds, will not spin up or is too slow, and you want a quiet, reliable replacement.

Before you start

  • Unplug before opening. Do not put cards in while powered on.

Board revisions and models

Older machines have limits on drive size and need a suitable driver or Kickstart/BIOS.

Step by step

  1. Back up data If the old drive works at all, copy data off first.
  2. Choose an adapter and card Pick an IDE adapter that fits your connector (3.5 inch or 2.5 inch) and a card of the size your machine can handle.
  3. Check limits Check your machine's BIOS or OS for the largest drive it can use.
  4. Open the machine Power off, unplug and open the case.
  5. Remove the old drive Unplug the cables and remove the screws.
  6. Fit the adapter Mount the adapter, connect the IDE ribbon and power.
  7. Set master and slave Set the master or slave jumper to match the old drive if needed.
  8. Configure the BIOS Detect the drive in setup.
  9. Install the system Install the OS or copy an image.
