PS2 will not power on: internal power supply checks

The internal supply is a common failure point. Work only with the console unplugged and stop if live testing is needed.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Power · Expert · about 90 minutes. Try it, but check first. This will not fix every machine, so read the sources first.

You are in the right place if: The console is completely dead: no light, no fan and no response.

Before you start

  • ConsoleMods warns probing a live power supply risks lethal electrocution.
  • Only work unplugged. If live testing is needed, seek a professional.
  • Do not work on supplies if you are not confident.

Board revisions and models

Procedure is for the fat PS2 internal supply.

Step by step

  1. Unplug and wait Remove the cord and wait before opening.
  2. Disassemble Open the case and remove the supply.
  3. Visual check Look for blackened components.
  4. Test the power cord Check continuity.
  5. Test the power switch Check continuity with the switch operated.
  6. Check the fuse Test fuse continuity.
  7. Inspect capacitors Look for bulging or crystalline residue. C20 and C3 are common failures.
  8. Replace failed parts Match capacitance and use equal or higher voltage.
  9. Reassemble and test Refit and power up.
