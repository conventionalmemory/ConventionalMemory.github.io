Replace Dreamcast VMU batteries

A VMU uses two coin cells that drain even when the unit sleeps, so a flat pair is the usual cause of a dead VMU.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Battery · Easy · about 10 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The VMU screen is blank, the clock resets, or it loses its settings when removed from the controller.

Before you start

  • Do not mix old and new cells.
  • Rechargeable LiPo conversions involve opening the VMU and soldering. ConsoleMods covers this, but it is an advanced mod.

Parts you may need

  • CR2032 battery two-pack. VMU needs two Amazon · eBay

Step by step

  1. Remove the VMU Pull it from the controller or console.
  2. Open the battery door Flip it over and open the cover on the back.
  3. Note orientation Check which way the cells face before removing them.
  4. Replace both cells Fit two fresh CR2032 batteries. Replace both at the same time.
  5. Clean contacts if needed If it still does not wake up, wipe the battery contacts with an isopropyl alcohol swab.
  6. Reinstall and test Set the clock, plug it into a controller and confirm the screen and saves work.
