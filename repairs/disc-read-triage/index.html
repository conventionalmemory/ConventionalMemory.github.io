Disc will not read: disc, lens, mechanism or laser?

A stepwise flow so you do not replace a laser that was fine. Cheap steps first, laser last.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Optical · Careful · about 60 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The console spins the disc, hunts, then gives up or reports an error.

Before you start

  • Never look into the laser.
  • Mark laser pots before moving them.

Step by step

  1. Test with a known-good original disc Burned and scratched discs confuse diagnosis.
  2. Listen and watch Does it spin, does the laser move, does the tray work? Different symptoms point to different faults.
  3. Clean the lens Isopropyl alcohol on a cotton swab.
  4. Check mechanical parts Look for dry lubricant, a warped belt or a broken gear.
  5. Lubricate and replace belts Replace stiff belts and lightly lubricate.
  6. Look at the drive board Capacitors on the drive PCB are often the cause. hitmanmcc notes it is usually the caps, not the laser.
  7. Consider the motor Check whether the spindle motor spins and measure it if you can.
  8. Tune the laser pot last Mark the position, change in tiny steps and revert if you hear rubbing.
  9. Replace the laser If all else fails, replace it. Optical drive emulators are a common alternative.

Guides we used

We wrote the steps in our own words from these. They have the photos and the full detail, so please read them and support their authors.

  • Symptom: Disc Read Error (Blog) by hitmanmcc (Hitman's Museum). Index of disc read repairs across consoles, with the note that it is often caps not the laser.
  • Technique: Optical Drive Service (Blog) by hitmanmcc (Hitman's Museum). Overview of lens, lubrication, belt, gear and pot tuning work.
  • Disc Drive Repair (Wiki) by ConsoleMods Wiki. General list of disc drive faults, lens cleaning, laser pots and lubricant, not console specific.
  • PS3 CECHB00 Disc Read Fix (Blog) by hitmanmcc (Hitman's Museum). PS3 tray that accepted discs but did not read them, fixed by lubrication and lens cleaning.
  • Sega CD laser adjustment thread (Forum) by Reddit r/SegaCD via mirror. Owner who replaced belt, battery and laser then tuned the laser with a multimeter.

Boards and models vary, so check what is in front of you. A repair can involve hot tools, chemicals and, on some machines, dangerous voltages. You do it at your own risk.
