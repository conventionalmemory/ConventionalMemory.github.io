Repair a GameCube controller: dead buttons, drift and worn caps

Carbon contacts and stick modules wear. Cleaning the contacts and replacing the stick fixes most worn controllers.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Controller · Medium · about 60 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: Buttons feel dead, the stick drifts or the rubber stick cover is crumbling.

Before you start

  • Do not use abrasives on carbon pads.
  • Take care desoldering the stick to avoid lifting traces.

Step by step

  1. Test the controller Check which buttons or axes fail.
  2. Open it up Remove the screws and carefully lift the shell.
  3. Photograph and sort parts Keep parts organised.
  4. Clean everything Wash the shell parts and wipe the board with alcohol.
  5. Restore the carbon contacts Clean the carbon contacts and pads with contact cleaner.
  6. Replace the stick if needed Desolder the analogue stick and fit a new one.
  7. Replace degraded stick covers Swap perished stick covers for fresh ones.
  8. Reassemble and test Check all buttons, triggers and the stick's full range.
