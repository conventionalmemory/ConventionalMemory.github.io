Swap or upgrade the original Xbox hard drive

The stock Xbox hard drive is locked to the console by a unique key. A swap means cloning the drive and locking the new one correctly.

Other · Careful · about 60 minutes. Try it, but check first. This will not fix every machine, so read the sources first.

You are in the right place if: The Xbox hard drive is clicking, failing or full, or you want a bigger drive.

Before you start

  • Do not format or replace the drive before backing up the EEPROM.
  • This is a software-and-hardware job. Follow a complete, current guide for your exact setup.

Board revisions and models

Modded consoles have different HDD requirements to stock systems.

Step by step

  1. Work out whether your Xbox is modded Modded and stock consoles have different drive requirements.
  2. Back up the EEPROM Save the EEPROM.bin file for future reference. Without it you may not be able to unlock a replacement drive later.
  3. Back up the drive contents Copy everything from the old drive before touching it.
  4. Choose a replacement drive A PATA drive or a PATA adapter for modern storage.
  5. Clone and lock the drive Use a tool like Chimp to copy the data and lock the new drive with the console's unique key.
  6. Use a good IDE cable An 80-wire PATA cable gives better stability with newer drives.
  7. Boot and verify Boot the console and confirm the dashboard and saves are intact.
  8. Keep backups Store the EEPROM backup and drive image somewhere safe.
