Xbox 360 will not read discs: belt, lens and laser service

A clogged drive, a warped belt and a tired laser can all stop a 360 from reading. A full service with cleaning and a belt or laser replacement fixed one documented unit.

You are in the right place if: The console fails to recognise most discs and the tray may be slow or reluctant to eject.

Before you start

  • Do not use a heat gun or other fixes that heat the whole board unless you know what you are doing.

Board revisions and models

Xbox 360, Elite and S each use different drive assemblies.

Step by step

  1. Test with several discs Try both original game discs and a simple data disc.
  2. Disassemble the console Remove the case parts, drive and fan assembly, keeping screws organised.
  3. Clean out dust Blow out dust from the console and the drive.
  4. Check the belt A warped belt can mimic a dead drive. Replace it, or as a stopgap boil it to restore shape.
  5. Clean the lens Use isopropyl alcohol and a cotton swab.
  6. Lubricate the mechanism Lightly lubricate the tray and sled mechanism with a plastic-safe grease.
  7. Refresh the thermal paste If the console is open, renew the paste.
  8. Replace the laser if needed Fit a new pickup if the old one is not recovering.
  9. Reassemble and test Run several games for an extended session.

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us.

  • Story (Medium confidence). One 360 that read discs about 1% of the time was cured by cleaning dust, reshaping the belt by boiling, cleaning the lens, new paste and a replacement laser. Source
