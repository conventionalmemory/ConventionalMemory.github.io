Read PC POST beeps and fix a no-video start

The motherboard BIOS beeps in patterns to say what failed. Note the pattern and BIOS vendor, then check the likely culprits such as RAM, video card and power.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Diagnosis · Easy · about 30 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The PC gives no picture, beeps in a pattern, or stops before the operating system loads.

Before you start

  • Unplug before reseating parts.
  • If you see bulged capacitors, do not keep powering the board.

Board revisions and models

Beep patterns differ between AMI, Award, Phoenix and IBM BIOS.

Step by step

  1. Note the beeps Write down the exact pattern, long and short, and how many.
  2. Identify the BIOS Look for the BIOS maker on the board or in the manual, since each has its own table.
  3. Look up the pattern Compare with the vendor's chart.
  4. Strip back to minimum Remove everything except CPU, one RAM module, video card and keyboard.
  5. Reseat RAM and cards Reseat the RAM and video card.
  6. Check the power supply Check the supply voltages.
  7. Check for bulged capacitors Look at the board for bulged or leaking capacitors.
  8. Check the battery Check the CMOS battery and reset the CMOS if needed.
  9. Add parts back Add parts back one at a time.
