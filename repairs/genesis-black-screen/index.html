Repair guide

Genesis or Mega Drive black screen with the power light on

Work from the cheapest checks, a dirty cartridge connector, up to power rails and board damage. Most cases end at the first or second step.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Diagnosis · Medium · about 45 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The power LED is lit but you get a black screen, no matter which game or cable you try.

Before you start

  • Do not use abrasives on cartridge pins.
  • Reflowing large chips is advanced work. Stop if you are not comfortable.

Board revisions and models

Model 1, 2 and 3 boards differ. Check the ConsoleMods motherboard differences page before probing.

Step by step

  1. Try another cart and cable Use a known-good cartridge and a known-good AV cable. If you have a Sega CD, try it, because it uses a separate connector.
  2. Clean the cartridge slot Remove the cover, clean the slot with an alcohol cloth wrapped around a card, and clean the cartridge edge too.
  3. Retension the cart port On heavily used Model 1 units, gently retensioning the cartridge connector helped some owners.
  4. Try another power supply The console may power on but not receive enough current. Test with a known-good supply.
  5. Inspect solder joints Open the console and look for cracked joints on external connectors.
  6. Check for broken traces Look near the cartridge slot for physical damage and use a multimeter for continuity.
  7. Probe the large chips Gently probe large chips such as the ASIC with tweezers to find loose pins and reflow if needed.
  8. Measure the 5V rail Check the output of the 7805 regulator. It should read 5V.
  9. Stop if no cause is found Further work needs an oscilloscope and component-level skills.

Tips, tales and maybes

Found in write-ups and forum threads. These are other people's reports, not tested by us.

  • Tip (Medium confidence). ConsoleMods suggests testing with a Sega CD to confirm the cartridge connector is the issue. Source
  • Story (Low confidence). One forum poster reported success with retensioning the cartridge port on well-used Model 1 consoles. Source
