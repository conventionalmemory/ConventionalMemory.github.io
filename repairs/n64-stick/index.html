Fix or replace a worn N64 controller stick

Try the no-tools re-centring tricks first, then swap the stick assembly if it is truly worn.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Controller · Easy · about 20 minutes. Worth doing. Reports say this fix helps on many machines.

You are in the right place if: The character drifts when you let go of the stick, or the stick feels loose and cannot reach full range.

Before you start

  • Aftermarket sticks can feel different. ConsoleMods notes at least one popular option has less smooth travel and a noticeable deadzone.
  • Keep screws sorted. The controller uses more than one length.

Step by step

  1. Test the drift Check in a menu or a test screen to confirm the controller, not the game, is at fault.
  2. Re-centre at power-up Make sure the stick is straight and untouched when you switch the console on, because the N64 uses its start position as the reference.
  3. Unplug and replug Hold the stick where it does not drift, unplug the controller and plug it back in to reset the zero point.
  4. Use L+R+Start In games that support it, hold the stick in a good position and press L, R and Start together to re-zero.
  5. Open the controller If those fail, remove the seven screws on the back.
  6. Remove the stick assembly Take out the three screws holding it and unplug its cable from the board.
  7. Fit the new assembly Install the replacement in the same place and plug in its cable.
  8. Close and test Reassemble and test the full range of motion.
