PS1 disc read errors: lens clean, drive check and laser pot

A dirty lens or worn mechanism is the easy fix. If that does not work, mark and tune the laser pot in tiny steps and revert if you hear rubbing.

Open the interactive checklist Tick off each step as you go. It saves in your browser.

Optical · Careful · about 60 minutes. Try it, but check first. This will not fix every machine, so read the sources first.

You are in the right place if: The PS1 shows a disc-read error, spins up then stops or fails to recognise discs.

Before you start

  • Never look into the laser.
  • Never turn the pot without marking where it started.

Board revisions and models

PSone and original PS1 differ. ConsoleMods names PSone capacitor C705 for disc detection issues.

Step by step

  1. Try other discs Use clean, original discs.
  2. Check the lid sensor Make sure the lid switch is detected closed and the disc spins freely.
  3. Clean the lens Open the lid and wipe the lens with alcohol on a cotton swab.
  4. Inspect the mechanism Look for dried lubricant, a stuck sled or a broken gear.
  5. Lubricate lightly Apply plastic-safe grease where metal rails and gears meet.
  6. Mark the laser pot Photograph and mark the pot's original position.
  7. Tune in tiny steps Turn a very small amount, test with a disc and repeat.
  8. Revert if the disc rubs Return the pot to its mark if the disc scrapes after tuning.
  9. Escalate only if needed The drive may need a new laser or other repairs.
