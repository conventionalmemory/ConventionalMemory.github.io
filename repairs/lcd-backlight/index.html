Work out why an LCD or laptop screen is dark or dim

A faint picture usually means the backlight, its inverter or its cable has failed, not the LCD itself. Narrowing it down takes a few outside checks and a torch.

You are in the right place if: The screen shows a very faint image, is dark, or flickers and dies.

Before you start

  • Inverter boards for CCFL backlights produce high voltage; do not touch them while powered.
  • Do not open a lid with a hinge cable unless you know the routing.
  • If a screen smells burnt, unplug it immediately.

Board revisions and models

CCFL, plasma and early LED backlights differ; check your model.

Step by step

  1. Look for a faint image In a dark room, shine a torch at the screen and see if there is a picture.
  2. Check brightness settings Adjust brightness and try the keys or controls.
  3. Test an external display If the device has a video-out, connect another display.
  4. Check the lid or hinge switch On laptops, a faulty lid switch can turn the backlight off.
  5. Check cables and connectors Check the display cable is seated if you can reach it safely.
  6. Suspect the inverter or lamp A faint image with no backlight points at the inverter or lamp.
  7. Replace inverter or lamp Replace the inverter or lamp only if you are experienced; they carry high voltage.
  8. Stop if unsure Seek a repair person for inverter and lamp work.
