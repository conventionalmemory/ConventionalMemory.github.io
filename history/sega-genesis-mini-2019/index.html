Sega Genesis Mini (2019)

A half-size Genesis/Mega Drive with 42 games, emulated by M2 after Sega parted from AtGames.

Launched September 19, 2019 at $79.99, with Europe delayed to October 4. It carried 40 Genesis games plus Darius and Tetris bonuses and used an ARM Cortex-A7 SoC. M2, known for Sega Ages, did the emulation, adding save states and widescreen. Outside North America it is the Mega Drive Mini. It sold over 300,000 units by March 2020.
