Sony Digital Mavica MVC-FD88 (1999)

1280x960 Digital Mavica with 8x optical zoom.

The FD88 was a higher-resolution floppy Mavica with 1280x960 stills and an 8x optical zoom. It also recorded short MPEG-1 clips. It is one of the best known floppy Mavicas among collectors.
