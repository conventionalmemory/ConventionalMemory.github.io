Duke Nukem: Time to Kill (1998)

Third-person PlayStation spin-off across three eras by n-Space.

Time to Kill is a Tomb Raider-style third-person shooter in which Duke travels through the Old West, Medieval Europe and Ancient Rome to stop the Draks. It features a two-player deathmatch mode and pop culture references. It scored 75% on GameRankings and was re-released on Evercade in 2023.
