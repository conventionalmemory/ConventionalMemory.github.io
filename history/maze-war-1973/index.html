Maze War (1973)

First-person multiplayer maze game started at NASA Ames in 1973 by Steve Colley, Greg Thompson and Howard Palmer. Described as the earliest first-person shooter.

Created 1973 to 1974 on the Imlac PDS-1, with an expanded MIT version on a PDP-10 and a 1977 Xerox Alto version.
