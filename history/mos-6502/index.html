MOS 6502 (1975)

MOS Technology introduced the 6502 in September 1975 at $25, far cheaper than the 6800 or 8080. It later powered the Apple II, Commodore PET and Atari 2600.

Introduced September 1975 at $25, less than one-sixth the price of rival designs from larger companies.
