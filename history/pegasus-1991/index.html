Pegasus (1991)

A Micro Genius-built Famiclone that was the gold standard of 8-bit gaming in Poland and neighbouring countries.

Sold in the Czech Republic, Poland, Indonesia, Bosnia-Herzegovina and Yugoslavia, the first model (MT-777DX) was very popular in 1992 and 1993. It was bundled with a 168-in-1 Contra multicart and offered cheat trainers. In 1994 BobMark got licences from Codemasters, Sachen and Western Technologies, and released variants such as Super Pegasus and the Mega Drive clone Power Pegasus.
