Apple Xserve (2002)

Apple's first rack-mounted server was introduced on May 14, 2002 and shipped in June. A single-processor model cost $2,999 and a dual-processor model $3,999.

1U server with one or two PowerPC G4 CPUs at 1 to 1.33 GHz, dual Gigabit Ethernet and Mac OS X Server.
