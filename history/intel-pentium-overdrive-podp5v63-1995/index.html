Intel Pentium OverDrive PODP5V63 (1995)

A Pentium-based upgrade chip for 486 boards.

Introduced 3 February 1995 as a 63 MHz chip on a 25 MHz bus. It had a 486-compatible bus unit and let owners upgrade without a new board.
