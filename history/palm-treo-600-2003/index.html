Palm Treo 600 (2003)

Handspring-built smartphone sold under the palmOne brand after the Palm and Handspring merger. It had a 144 MHz OMAP1510 processor, Palm OS 5 and a thumb keyboard.

Color touchscreen Palm OS smartphone with a 2.5 inch 160x160 display and 32 MB of RAM.
