Apple iMac (Intel Core Duo) (2006)

First Intel-based iMac, announced January 10, 2006 with an Intel Core Duo CPU, 512 MB of RAM and a 17 or 20 inch display.

The first Intel iMac, introduced at Macworld 2006 and shipped with Mac OS X 10.4 Tiger.
