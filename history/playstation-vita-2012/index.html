PlayStation Vita (2012)

Sony's powerful quad-core handheld with an OLED screen and rear touchpad. Launch price and date are for North America; Japan got it on 2011-12-17.

Vita launched in Japan on 17 December 2011. Strong hardware but pricey proprietary cards and slow software hampered it.
