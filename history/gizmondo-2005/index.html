Gizmondo (2005)

A GPS and camera-equipped UK handheld that collapsed after a scandal.

Launched in March 2005, Gizmondo included GPS, camera, Bluetooth and ad-supported games. Tiger Telematics collapsed within a year amid a high-profile fraud case.
