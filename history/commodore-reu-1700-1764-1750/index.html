Commodore REU (1700/1764/1750) (1986)

A RAM Expansion Unit in 128, 256 and 512 KB sizes for the C64 and C128.

Used a DMA RAM Expansion Controller. GEOS used it as a very fast RAM disk, and BBS and disk-copy software exploited the speed. The 1700 had 128 KB, the 1764 256 KB and the 1750 512 KB.
