Dycam Model 1 (1991)

One of the first digital cameras sold to consumers, also sold as the Logitech Fotoman.

A small grayscale camera that stored a few dozen low-resolution images in its own memory and downloaded them to a PC or Mac over a serial cable. It was priced for hobbyists rather than professionals and used Dycam's own software for transfer. Logitech rebranded it as the Fotoman. Collectors hunt for it as a pre-QuickTake, pre-USB oddity.
