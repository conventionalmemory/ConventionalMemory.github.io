Tapwave Zodiac (2003)

A Palm OS PDA/handheld with a gaming focus and analog controls.

The Zodiac ran Palm OS, had an ARM CPU and a big screen. It had strong specs for its time but was discontinued by 2005 when Tapwave failed.
