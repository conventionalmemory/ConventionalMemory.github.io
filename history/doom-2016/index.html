Doom (2016) (2016)

Reboot with fast-paced combat; Switch port by Panic Button.

Directed by Marty Stratton and Hugo Martin. PC sales reached 500,000 by May 2016 and 2 million by July 2017. Metacritic 85 PC/PS4, 87 Xbox One, 79 Switch. Mick Gordon's score won at The Game Awards 2016. Vulkan was added in July 2016.
