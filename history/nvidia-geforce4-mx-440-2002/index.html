NVIDIA GeForce4 MX 440 (2002)

Launched on February 6, 2002 alongside the Ti 4600 as a mass-market GeForce4 part. It was a refined GeForce2-class design without pixel shaders.

Entry GeForce4 model aimed at OEM and budget systems.
