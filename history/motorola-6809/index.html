Motorola 6809 (1978)

Motorola introduced the 6809 in 1978, an 8-bit processor with some 16-bit features. It powered the TRS-80 Color Computer, Dragon 32 and Vectrex.

Introduced in 1978. Used in the TRS-80 Color Computer, Dragon 32/64, Vectrex and Williams arcade games such as Defender.
