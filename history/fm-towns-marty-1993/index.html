FM Towns Marty (1993)

Fujitsu's console version of its FM Towns PC, billed as the first 32-bit CD console.

Released at 98,000 yen with an AMD 386SX, 2 MB RAM and CD-ROM plus floppy, with FM Towns software compatibility. About 45,000 sold in 1993; the Marty 2 cut the price in 1994 but Fujitsu abandoned the line. Its software is now rare and costly.
