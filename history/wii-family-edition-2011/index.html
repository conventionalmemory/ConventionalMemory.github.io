Wii Family Edition (2011)

A cheaper, upright-or-flat Wii (RVL-101) with no GameCube support.

Wikipedia lists the RVL-101 Wii Family Edition as dropping GameCube controller ports, memory card slots and GameCube backward compatibility, priced at $149.99. The original Wii launched in North America on November 19, 2006 at $249.99 and sold over 101 million units.
