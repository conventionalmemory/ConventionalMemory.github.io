Creative Wave Blaster (1993)

The wavetable daughterboard that made Sound Blaster 16 cards a General MIDI synth.

Attached to the Wave Blaster header on SB16-class cards. Wave Blaster II later used the E-mu EMU8000 engine. Header adopted by Roland, Yamaha, Turtle Beach, Ensoniq and others.
