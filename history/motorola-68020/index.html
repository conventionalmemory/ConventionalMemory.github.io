Motorola 68020 (1984)

32-bit Motorola processor announced in June 1984 at 487 dollars per unit, with about 200,000 transistors and clock speeds from 12 to 33 MHz.

The 68020 followed the 68000 and 68010 and was produced at 12 MHz to 33 MHz.
