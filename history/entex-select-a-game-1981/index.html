Entex Select-A-Game (1981)

A tabletop/handheld console with a VFD screen and static overlays, with a microprocessor in each cartridge.

Released in 1981 with Space Invader 2 bundled, it had no CPU of its own. Titles included Basketball 3, Football 4, Pinball and Baseball 4. Pacman 2 was pulled after Coleco sued over handheld Pac-Man rights. Entex dropped it in 1982 for the Adventure Vision. The concept resembled Milton Bradley's Microvision.
