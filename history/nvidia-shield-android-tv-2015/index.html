Nvidia Shield Android TV (2015)

A 4K Android TV box focused on gaming and GeForce Now.

Launched in North America May 28, 2015 with a Tegra X1 and 3 GB RAM, in 16 GB and 500 GB (Pro) forms. It streams games from GeForce Now and a PC. Updates through 2025.
