Palm VII (1999)

Palm organizer with integrated wireless Web clipping via Palm.net.

The Palm VII added integrated wireless Web clipping through the Palm.net service in the US. At US$599 it was the most expensive Palm sold to date when it launched in May 1999. It ran Palm OS 3.2.
