Duke Nukem Trilogy: Critical Mass (2011)

Frontline Studios' DS shooter, Europe only.

Critical Mass is a run-and-gun with about 36 levels and third, first and top-down views. It was planned to be renamed Extraction Point without the license. A PSP version never launched but its source leaked in 2022. Metacritic rated it generally unfavorable.
