Master Levels for Doom II (1995)

Commercial add-on with 21 levels by community designers for Doom II.

Master Levels for Doom II was released in December 1995 and added 21 commercial levels created by outside designers. Wikipedia's Doom II article also notes more than 3,000 user-created options by then.
