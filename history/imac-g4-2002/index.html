iMac G4 (2002)

Apple's flat-panel iMac on an adjustable arm, introduced on January 7, 2002. It was the first Mac to boot into Mac OS X by default.

Dome base with a swivel flat-panel display, available with 15, 17 and later 20 inch screens, and PowerPC G4 processors from 700 MHz to 1.25 GHz.
