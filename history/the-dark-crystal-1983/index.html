The Dark Crystal (1983)

Sierra On-Line Hi-Res Adventure based on the Jim Henson film, released in 1983 for Apple II and Atari 8-bit as the first SierraVenture title.

Tie-in to the 1982 Jim Henson film, part of Sierra's Hi-Res Adventure line.
