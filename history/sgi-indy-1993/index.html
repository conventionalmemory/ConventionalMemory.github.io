SGI Indy (1993)

Silicon Graphics desktop workstation with a MIPS R4000 and a built-in IndyCam video camera. It was the first computer to ship with a camera as standard.

Desktop workstation that included an IndyCam video camera as standard, with video inputs called Vino.
