Sony ProMavica MVC-2000 (1989)

Professional analog Mavica that was pre-production tested in 1986.

The ProMavica line targeted professionals such as news photographers. Wikipedia notes pre-production testing of the MVC-2000 in 1986, ahead of its 1989 release. It used the Mavipak disk with Hi-Band recording.
