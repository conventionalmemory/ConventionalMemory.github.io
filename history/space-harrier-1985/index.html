Space Harrier (1985)

Rail shooter designed by Yu Suzuki for Sega arcades, released October 2, 1985 in Japan.

Space Harrier began as a military flight simulation and was redesigned with a fantasy setting. Yu Suzuki was inspired by The NeverEnding Story.
