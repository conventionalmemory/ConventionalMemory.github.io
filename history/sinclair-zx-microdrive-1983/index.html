Sinclair ZX Microdrive (1983)

A cheap tape-loop cartridge drive for the ZX Spectrum and QL.

Launched at 49.95 pounds with about 85 KB per cartridge and a 15 KB/s transfer rate, much faster than cassette. Reliability was poor.
