Canon EOS D60 (2002)

6.3 MP prosumer DSLR that replaced the D30.

The D60 doubled the D30's resolution to 6.3 MP and shot at up to 5.4 frames per second in bursts. It used CompactFlash and USB, and was replaced by the 10D. It sold at $1,999 for the body.
