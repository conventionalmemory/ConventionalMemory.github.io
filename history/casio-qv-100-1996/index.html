Casio QV-100 (1996)

Casio's second consumer digital camera, a QV-10 follow-up.

A successor to the QV-10, the QV-100 added more resolution and kept the LCD-on-the-back idea. It connected to PCs by serial and used Casio's own software. It extended Casio's early lead in consumer digital cameras.
