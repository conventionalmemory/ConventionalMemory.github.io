Nikon D90 (2008)

12.3 MP DSLR, the first DSLR with video recording.

Announced Aug 27, 2008 at $899.95 body-only, the D90 recorded 720p video at 24 fps in mono. It used SD/SDHC cards. Its movie mode helped start the DSLR video era.
