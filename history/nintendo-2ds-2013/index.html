Nintendo 2DS (2013)

A slate-shaped, non-3D budget 3DS aimed at younger children.

Released October 12, 2013 at $129.99, it removes the 3D display and clamshell hinge. It reached Japan only on February 27, 2016. The New Nintendo 2DS XL arrived in 2017 and was the last 3DS-family console in production, to September 2020. About 10.29 million 2DS units shipped by March 2019.
