Nintendo GameCube (Japan) (2001)

The Japanese launch of Nintendo's compact, disc-based console.

Nintendo's first optical-disc console debuted in Japan on 14 September 2001. It used proprietary 8 cm mini-DVDs and a handled box. Sales totalled about 21 million units.
