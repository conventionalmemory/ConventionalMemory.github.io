Epson FX-80 (1983)

The MX-80's replacement: faster, wider platen, bigger buffer and user-definable characters.

Epson FX command compatibility became a baseline that nearly every DOS word processor and spreadsheet drove. It stayed the workhorse for home and office text until laser printers got cheap.
