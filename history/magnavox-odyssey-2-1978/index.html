Magnavox Odyssey 2 (1978)

The cartridge-based Odyssey successor with a built-in membrane keyboard; sold as the Philips Videopac in Europe.

K.C. Munchkin! was a massive hit that prompted Atari's landmark copyright suit. It also launched in Europe in December 1978, Japan in 1982 and Brazil in 1983.
