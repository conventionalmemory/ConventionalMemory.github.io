Power Macintosh G3 (Beige) (1997)

Apple's first Power Macintosh with a PowerPC G3 processor, sold in desktop, minitower and all-in-one cases. Speeds ran from 233 to 333 MHz.

The first Macs with the PowerPC 750 (G3) chip, shipping with Mac OS 8. The Blue and White model replaced it in 1999.
