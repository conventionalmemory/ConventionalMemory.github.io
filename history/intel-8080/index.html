Intel 8080 (1974)

Intel's 8-bit processor launched in April 1974. It powered the Altair 8800 and was the original target CPU for CP/M.

Launched April 1974. Used in the Altair 8800, IMSAI 8080 and Sol-20, and the basis of the x86 instruction set lineage.
