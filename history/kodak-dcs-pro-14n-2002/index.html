Kodak DCS Pro 14n (2002)

14 MP full-frame DSLR on a Nikon F80 body. Announced at photokina in September 2002; production models shipped in May 2003.

Kodak's last big DCS, it used a 14 MP FillFactory full-frame CMOS sensor and saved to CompactFlash. It was known for noisy high-ISO output and slow operation, but delivered big files for the price. It linked to computers via FireWire.
