Akalabeth: World of Doom (1980)

Richard Garriott's first RPG. Sold at $20 in Ziploc bags, then $35 from California Pacific. MS-DOS re-release on February 17, 1998.

Written in Applesoft BASIC by Garriott as a high-school project; it led directly to the Ultima series.
