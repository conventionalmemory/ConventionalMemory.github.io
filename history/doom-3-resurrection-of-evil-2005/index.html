Doom 3: Resurrection of Evil (2005)

Expansion adding the Grabber and Artifact, set two years after Doom 3.

Nerve Software developed Resurrection of Evil, announced in October 2004. It adds the Grabber, a gravity-gun like tool, and the Artifact with hell time slow-motion. It scored 78/100 on Metacritic for PC and 77 for Xbox. The Xbox version attaches the flashlight to a weapon.
