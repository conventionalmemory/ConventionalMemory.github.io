NES Zapper (1985)

Nintendo's light gun, bundled with Duck Hunt.

It blanked the screen for a frame then drew targets white so a photodiode could detect hits. It worked only on CRT TVs. Duck Hunt, Wild Gunman and Hogan's Alley used it.
