Nokia N-Gage QD (2004)

A slimmer redesign of the N-Gage that moved the cartridge slot and earpiece but dropped MP3 and radio.

Released May 26, 2004, it fixed the 'taco phone' complaint about side-talking. The original N-Gage launched October 7, 2003 at $299, and about 2 million units were sold across both models by 2007.
