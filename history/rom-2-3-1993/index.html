ROM 2.3 (1993)

Russ Taylor's Rom 2.3, the Merc derivative used by MUDs such as Aardwolf.

ROM was released by Russ Taylor in 1993 as a Merc derivative, and in 1994 he formed the Rom Consortium. ROM 2.4 became a widely used base, with 2.4b6 in May 1998 as the last public version.
