Nintendo DSi (2009)

A DS revision with cameras, SD slot and a downloadable DSiWare store. Launch price and date are for the United States; Japan got it on 2008-11-01.

DSi launched in Japan on 1 November 2008 with two cameras and an online shop, dropping the GBA slot.
