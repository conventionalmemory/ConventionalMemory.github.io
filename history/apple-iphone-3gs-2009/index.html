Apple iPhone 3GS (2009)

Faster third iPhone with 256 MB of RAM and a 600 MHz Cortex-A8 CPU. It shipped with iPhone OS 3.0 and offered 8, 16 or 32 GB of storage.

The third iPhone model, released June 19, 2009 with a faster CPU and more memory than the iPhone 3G.
