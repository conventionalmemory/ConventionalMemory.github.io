Kodak DCS 200 (1992)

Kodak and Nikon digital SLR with a 1.5 MP sensor and a separate hard drive unit.

Built on a Nikon F-801s body with a KAF-1600 sensor, the DCS 200 stored images on an 80 MB hard drive in a bolt-on unit. Photojournalists used it with SCSI links to Macs. It improved on the DCS 100, and is a key piece of the early professional DSLR story.
