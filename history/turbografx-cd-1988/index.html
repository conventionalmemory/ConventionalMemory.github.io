TurboGrafx-CD (1988)

CD-ROM add-on for the PC Engine, the first CD-based console system. Japan launch (as PC Engine CD-ROM2) was December 4, 1988; the US TurboGrafx-CD followed in November 1989.

Dated Dec 1988 per the list article; the $399.99 price is the NA launch. It helped define the format shift.
