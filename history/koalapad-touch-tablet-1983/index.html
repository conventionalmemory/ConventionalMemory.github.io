KoalaPad Touch Tablet (1983)

A low-cost touch graphics tablet designed for schools and home users.

Created by David Thornburg, the KoalaPad had a four-inch square pad you drew on with a stylus or a finger. It shipped with KoalaPainter software and was ported to the Apple II, TRS-80 Color Computer, Atari 8-bit, C64 and PC.
