Final Doom (1996)

Two 32-level megawads, TNT: Evilution and The Plutonia Experiment, for Doom II.

Final Doom is the first Doom product not developed by id itself. TNT: Evilution by TeamTNT was originally to be free until Romero contacted them five hours before launch. The Plutonia Experiment was made by Dario and Milo Casali in four months at id's request. Reviews were mixed: GameSpot called it a waste of money given free fan maps, while PlayStation Magazine gave 9/10.
