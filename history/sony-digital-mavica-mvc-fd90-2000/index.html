Sony Digital Mavica MVC-FD90 (2000)

Floppy Mavica with external flash jack and manual focus.

The FD90 added an external flash connection and manual focus. Stills were 1280x960 recorded and upscaled to 1472x1104 files, plus MPEG-1 clips.
