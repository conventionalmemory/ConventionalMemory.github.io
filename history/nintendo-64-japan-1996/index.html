Nintendo 64 (Japan) (1996)

The Japanese launch of Nintendo's 64-bit console, three months before North America.

Launched June 23, 1996 with Super Mario 64 and Pilotwings 64; Nintendo 64 sold 32.93 million total. It used cartridges and an SGI-designed RCP.
