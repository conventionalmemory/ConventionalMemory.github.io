Nintendo Color TV-Game 6 (1977)

Nintendo's first video game system, a Pong-style dedicated console built with Mitsubishi.

The Color TV-Game 15 followed a week later, then Racing 112 (1978) and Block Kuzushi (1979), together selling three million. Its aggressively low pricing created Nintendo's philosophy of selling consoles at a profit and led to the Famicom.
