Bally Astrocade (1978)

A powerful but hard-to-program Z80 console that doubled as a BASIC computer.

It had capable graphics for its time but its architecture was hard to exploit and it sold poorly. Its library included Gunfight, Red Baron and arcade ports like Galaxian.
