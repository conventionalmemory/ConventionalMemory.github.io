Mario Bros. (1983)

Nintendo's two-player arcade game that introduced Luigi. It was shown at the AMOA show in March 1983 and went into mass production in Japan on June 21.

Designed by Shigeru Miyamoto and Gunpei Yokoi. It debuted in North America at the AMOA show in March 1983 and entered mass production in Japan on June 21.
