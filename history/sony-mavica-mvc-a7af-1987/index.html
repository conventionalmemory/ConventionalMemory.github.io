Sony Mavica MVC-A7AF (1987)

First commercial analog Mavica, recording still video frames to a 2-inch disk.

The ProMavica MVC-A7AF recorded analog video images onto 2-inch video floppy disks. It had a camcorder-like body with a 6x autofocus zoom lens and stored 25 frame images per 2-inch disk in frame mode.
