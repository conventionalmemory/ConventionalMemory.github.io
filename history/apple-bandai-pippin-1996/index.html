Apple Bandai Pippin (1996)

A Mac OS-based console that Apple licensed to Bandai, a quick failure. Japan launch March 1996 at 64,800 yen; US launch June 1996 at $599.

Bandai Atmark launched in Japan in March 1996 at 64,800 yen; Bandai @World hit the US in June 1996 at $599. A 66 MHz PowerPC 603 and 4x CD drive ran System 7.5.2. It had no big publishers and Steve Jobs ended it in 1997.
