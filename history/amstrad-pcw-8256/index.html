Amstrad PCW 8256 (1985)

Amstrad word processing computer sold as a bundle with monitor, printer, word processor and CP/M. It was priced at 399 pounds plus VAT at its British launch.

The PCW 8256 launched in Britain in September 1985 at 399 pounds plus VAT including the printer, a word processor, CP/M and Mallard BASIC.
