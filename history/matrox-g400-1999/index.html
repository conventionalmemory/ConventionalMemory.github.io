Matrox G400 (1999)

Matrox's gaming 3D flagship, famous for DualHead dual-monitor output.

One desktop could be spanned or cloned across two screens, and bump mapping (EMBM) was supported in Dungeon Keeper 2. Image quality was top-tier but drivers were weak at first.
