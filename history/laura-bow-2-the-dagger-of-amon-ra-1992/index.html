Laura Bow 2: The Dagger of Amon Ra (1992)

Sierra's second Laura Bow murder mystery, set in a 1920s New York museum.

A point-and-click whodunit in which cub reporter Laura Bow investigates a museum theft that turns into murder. The ending depends on how much evidence the player gathers. A CD-ROM version with voice acting followed in 1993.
