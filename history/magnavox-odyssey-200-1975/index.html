Magnavox Odyssey 200 (1975)

The first dedicated console with an option for four on-screen paddles.

Released November 12, 1975 at $129.95, it offered Tennis, Hockey and Smash, with a mechanical score slider rather than digital scoring and a Texas Instruments multi-chip design. It arrived just after the Odyssey 100 and was followed by the single-chip Odyssey 300 in October 1976. Magnavox sold 1,773,918 Odyssey-brand units between 1972 and 1981.
