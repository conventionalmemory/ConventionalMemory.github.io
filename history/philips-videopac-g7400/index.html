Philips Videopac+ G7400 (1983)

Philips' upgraded Odyssey 2 with a second graphics chip, sold in Europe from late 1983. The planned US version (Odyssey 3, $199) was cancelled.

Released in limited quantities in Europe only; the planned US Odyssey3 never appeared. It ran G7000 games and had a French clone.
