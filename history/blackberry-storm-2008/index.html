BlackBerry Storm (2008)

First touchscreen BlackBerry, released in the UK on November 14, 2008 and in the US on November 21. Its SurePress screen clicked like a button.

RIM's first touchscreen phone, made to compete with the iPhone. It was poorly reviewed.
