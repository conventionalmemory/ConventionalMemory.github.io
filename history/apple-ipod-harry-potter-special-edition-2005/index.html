Apple iPod Harry Potter Special Edition (2005)

A 4th-generation color-display iPod engraved with the Hogwarts logo and preloaded with the Harry Potter audiobooks.

This special edition shipped with the first six Harry Potter audiobooks preloaded and a Hogwarts logo engraved on the back. It was an early example of Apple's collaboration editions. It sat in the 4th generation color-display line sold from mid-2005.
