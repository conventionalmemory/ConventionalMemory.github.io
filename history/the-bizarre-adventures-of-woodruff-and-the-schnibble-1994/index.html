The Bizarre Adventures of Woodruff and the Schnibble (1994)

Quirky Coktel adventure by Pierre Gilhodes.

Woodruff, half-human and half-Bouzouk, tries to rescue his father in a dystopian vertical city. Its humor and colorful visuals recall the Gobliiins games.
