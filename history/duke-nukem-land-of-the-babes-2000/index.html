Duke Nukem: Land of the Babes (2000)

n-Space PlayStation sequel to Time to Kill.

Land of the Babes sends Duke to a dystopian future to join the Unified Babe Resistance against Silverback. It scored 37/100 on Metacritic and was re-released on Evercade in November 2023 in Duke Nukem Collection 2.
