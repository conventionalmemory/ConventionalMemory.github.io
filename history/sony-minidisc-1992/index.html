Sony MiniDisc (1992)

Sony's recordable mini optical disc, launched as a cassette replacement.

Announced September 1992 and shipped in November 1992 in Japan, December in Europe and North America. ATRAC compression cut a CD's 1.4 Mbit/s to 292 kbit/s. In 1993 MD Data stored 140 MB, a computer variant.
