PlayStation Classic (2018)

Sony's mini PlayStation with 20 games. It was widely panned and heavily discounted.

Released December 3, 2018 at $99.99, 24 years to the day after the Japanese PlayStation. It ran the PCSX ReARMed open-source emulator on a quad-core Cortex-A35 with 1 GB RAM. Critics disliked the game list, the use of slower PAL versions for nine games and the lack of analog sticks. It fell to $60 by Christmas and $20 by July 2019.
