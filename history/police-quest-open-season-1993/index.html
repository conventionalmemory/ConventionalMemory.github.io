Police Quest: Open Season (1993)

Daryl Gates' LAPD-flavored Police Quest 4 with realistic procedure.

Detective John Carey investigates murders in Los Angeles, and the player is penalized for procedure and paperwork errors. Retrospective reviews criticize its depiction of ethnic stereotypes.
