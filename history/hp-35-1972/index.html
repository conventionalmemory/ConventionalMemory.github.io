HP-35 (1972)

Hewlett-Packard's first pocket calculator and the first scientific pocket calculator, launched in 1972 at $395. It sold 100,000 in its first year.

Launched 1972 at $395, an IEEE Milestone, signalling the end of the slide rule for engineering students.
