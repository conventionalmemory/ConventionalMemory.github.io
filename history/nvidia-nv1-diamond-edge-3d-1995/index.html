Nvidia NV1 (Diamond Edge 3D) (1995)

Nvidia's first graphics product, sold as Diamond Edge 3D, combining 3D, sound and gamepad ports.

Its quadrupole-patch rendering clashed with DirectX, and Diamond wrote off $5 million in inventory in 1996.
