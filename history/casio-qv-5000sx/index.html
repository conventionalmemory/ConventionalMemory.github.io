Casio QV-5000SX (1998)

Higher-end Casio QV with better resolution and CompactFlash.

The QV-5000SX moved Casio to higher resolution and removable memory, aimed at keen amateurs. It connected via serial or USB depending on market, and is a late-1990s mid-priced classic.
