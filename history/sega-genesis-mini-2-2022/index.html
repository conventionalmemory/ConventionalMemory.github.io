Sega Genesis Mini 2 (2022)

The sequel mini with 60 games, mixing Genesis and Sega CD titles.

Released October 27, 2022 with 60 games (53 Genesis and Sega CD titles plus seven extras). M2 again handled emulation and Yuzo Koshiro returned for menu music. It is the Mega Drive Mini 2 in Japan and Europe.
