Knight Lore (1984)

Isometric action-adventure by Ultimate Play the Game for the ZX Spectrum. It was ported to the BBC Micro, Amstrad CPC and MSX in 1985 and 1986.

Knight Lore was released in Europe in November 1984 and was later ported to the Famicom Disk System.
