Magnavox Odyssey (1972)

The first commercial home video game console, built on Ralph Baer's design. It introduced the idea of playing games on a TV set at home.

Its cards did not hold software but rewired the console's internal circuits, and plastic overlays stuck to the TV supplied the visuals. A table tennis game seen on it inspired Atari's Pong, and Magnavox later won more than $100 million in patent litigation. It was followed by the Odyssey 100 and 200.
