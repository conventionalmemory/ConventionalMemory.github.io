iPod from HP (2004)

Apple's iPod sold under license as an HP-branded player through retailers.

Announced January 8, 2004, the HP-branded iPod was sold under license from Apple, with HP aiming to reach buyers through its retail channels including Walmart. It was essentially an Apple iPod with HP branding and bundled with iTunes. The arrangement ended in 2005. Collectors prize the HP logo units as an oddity of the iPod's early PC-market push.
