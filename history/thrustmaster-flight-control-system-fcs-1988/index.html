ThrustMaster Flight Control System (FCS) (1988)

Classic PC flight stick with a separate throttle (WCS) added later.

The FCS pioneered the stick plus throttle layout for PC flight sims, and the Weapon Control System throttle made hands-on control possible. Used for F-16 style simulators.
