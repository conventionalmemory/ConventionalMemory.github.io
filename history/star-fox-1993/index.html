Star Fox (1993)

Super NES space shooter from Nintendo EAD and Argonaut Software that used the Super FX chip for polygon 3D. The Super FX is described as the first 3D graphics accelerator in a consumer product.

Super NES shooter that used the Super FX chip for polygon graphics, described as the first 3D graphics accelerator in a consumer product. Japan February 21, 1993; North America March 23, 1993.
