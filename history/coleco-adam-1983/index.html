Coleco Adam (1983)

Home computer from Coleco built around the ColecoVision. It was sold as a complete system with a tape drive, daisy wheel printer and word processor, and was announced at a lower price.

Complete home computer system with 64 KB of RAM, Digital Data Pack tape drive, letter-quality printer, SmartWriter and SmartBASIC.
