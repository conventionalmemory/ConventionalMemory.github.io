ZX Spectrum+ (1984)

Restyled 48 KB ZX Spectrum with a full-size keyboard and a reset button. It kept the 48 KB memory of the original Spectrum.

The ZX Spectrum+ went on sale on 15 October 1984 at 179 pounds. It was a 48 KB Spectrum in a new case with an injection-molded keyboard.
