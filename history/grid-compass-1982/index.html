GRiD Compass (1982)

Early clamshell laptop with an electroluminescent display and magnetic bubble memory, introduced as the Compass 1101.

One of the first clamshell laptops, with an Intel 8086, 384 KB of bubble memory and a 320 x 240 pixel display.
