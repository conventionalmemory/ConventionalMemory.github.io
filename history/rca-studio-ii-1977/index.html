RCA Studio II (1977)

RCA's black-and-white cartridge console with built-in keypads.

Joyce Weisbecker, daughter of designer Joseph Weisbecker, became the first woman to develop a commercial video game on it. RCA ended the line in February 1978 after poor holiday sales.
