Jupiter Ace (1982)

British home computer from Jupiter Cantab that shipped with Forth instead of BASIC. It had 3 KB of RAM and a Z80 at 3.25 MHz.

Home computer with the Forth language (ACE Forth) in ROM rather than BASIC.
