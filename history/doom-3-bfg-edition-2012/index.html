Doom 3: BFG Edition (2012)

Remastered bundle of Doom 3, Resurrection of Evil, The Lost Mission, plus Doom and Doom II.

BFG Edition bundles Doom, Doom II, Doom 3, Resurrection of Evil and the new Lost Mission. Release dates vary: Australia October 15, North America October 16 and Europe October 19, 2012.
