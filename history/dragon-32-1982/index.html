Dragon 32 (1982)

Home computer from Dragon Data that used a Motorola 6809E and shipped with 32 KB of RAM. A 64 KB Dragon 64 followed.

British-built home computer based on the Motorola 6809E, sold with 32 KB (Dragon 32) or 64 KB (Dragon 64) of RAM.
