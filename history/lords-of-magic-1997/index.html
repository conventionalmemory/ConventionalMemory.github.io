Lords of Magic (1997)

Fantasy turn-based strategy with real-time battles.

Players lead one of eight factions in the land of Urak to defeat Balkoth, Lord of Death. It blends ideas from Heroes of Might and Magic II and Lords of the Realm II. A Special Edition followed in 1998.
