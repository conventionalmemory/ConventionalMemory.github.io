NewTek Video Toaster (1990)

A broadcast-quality video switcher, effects and 3D system on a card for the Amiga 2000.

It put TV-studio effects within reach of small stations, schools and hobbyists, and was used on NBC promos, The Tonight Show and Babylon 5 effects. About $5,000 for a turnkey system with the computer.
