Galaxy Game (1971)

Bill Pitts and Hugh Tuck's coin-operated Spacewar at Stanford's Tresidder Union, first installed in November 1971. It cost about $20,000 to build.

First installed November 1971 in Tresidder Union at Stanford. It ran on a PDP-11 with a Hewlett-Packard display and was never mass produced.
