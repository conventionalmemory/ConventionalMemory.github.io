Neo Geo Pocket Color (1999)

SNK's color handheld with a microswitched joystick, strong in fighters.

Japan March 19, 1999 at 8,900 yen; North America August 6 at $69.95; Europe October 1. 73 games released; discontinued after SNK's bankruptcy.
