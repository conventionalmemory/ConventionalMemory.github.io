Super A'Can (1995)

A Taiwanese 16-bit console that failed so badly it ruined its maker.

Launched October 25, 1995 at NT$2,900 with a 68000 CPU, and discontinued on April 4, 1996. Twelve games are confirmed. Funtech lost over $6 million and sold remaining units for parts.
