Commodore SX-64 (1983)

Portable version of the Commodore 64 with a built-in 5 inch color monitor and 1541 disk drive. Announced January 1983, it reached stores around the end of 1983 at $995.

Luggable Commodore 64 with a 5 inch color CRT.
