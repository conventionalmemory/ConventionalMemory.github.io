Chrono Trigger (1995)

Square's Super NES RPG by the Dragon Quest, Final Fantasy and Dragon Ball creators. Released in Japan on March 11, 1995 and in North America on August 22, 1995.

Built by the Dragon Quest and Final Fantasy creators' 'Dream Team' and shipped on a 32-megabit cartridge.
