PlayStation eXtra (PSX) (2003)

A Japan-only Sony set-top box combining a PlayStation 2, DVD recorder and hard drive, launched on December 13, 2003. Sony later canceled international plans and cut the price in 2004.

Hybrid with 160 GB or 250 GB hard drives, XrossMediaBar interface, and PlayStation and PlayStation 2 compatibility.
