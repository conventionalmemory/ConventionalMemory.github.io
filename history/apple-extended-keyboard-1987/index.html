Apple Extended Keyboard (1987)

Apple's full-size ADB keyboard with Alps mechanical switches and function keys.

Introduced at US$163, it was a high-end keyboard for Macintosh II and SE users and is highly sought by collectors. It had a continuous convex profile and a separate power key.
