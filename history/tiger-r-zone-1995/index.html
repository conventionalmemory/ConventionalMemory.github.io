Tiger R-Zone (1995)

A head-worn red LCD gadget made to ride Virtual Boy hype.

Released in 1995 at $29.99 with a transparent LCD projected onto a mirror. Four versions came out (Headgear, SuperScreen, DataZone and XPG) and it was discontinued by 1997. Games like Batman Forever and Mortal Kombat 3 were crude. It is widely called one of the worst consoles ever.
