TI-99/4A (1981)

Texas Instruments' revision of the TI-99/4 with a full-travel keyboard and lower price. It was the company's best-known home computer until TI left the market in 1983.

Home computer built around the TMS9900 CPU and TMS9918A video chip, priced at less than half the 99/4.
