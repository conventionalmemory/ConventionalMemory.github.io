Creative 3D Blaster PCI (Rendition Vérité V1000) (1996)

Creative's PCI board using Rendition's programmable V1000, one of the first affordable 3D cards.

Native support in Quake (vQuake), Tomb Raider, Descent II and Quake II demonstrated hardware 3D faster and better looking than software. The V1000 was eclipsed by Voodoo's raw speed.
