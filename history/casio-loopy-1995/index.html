Casio Loopy (1995)

A console marketed to girls, with a built-in thermal printer for making stickers from game screens.

Released only in Japan on October 19, 1995 at 25,000 yen with a Hitachi SH-1 class CPU. Eleven games came out, and the Magical Shop accessory captured video for stickers. Casio stopped production in December 1998.
