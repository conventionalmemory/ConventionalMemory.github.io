IMSAI 8080 (1975)

The first clone of the Altair 8800, with an S-100 bus and 22 slots. Released December 1975 at $439 as a kit or $621 assembled.

Released December 1975. Between 17,000 and 20,000 were made before IMSAI went bankrupt in 1979. It was featured in the film WarGames.
