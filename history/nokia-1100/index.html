Nokia 1100 (2003)

Announced on August 27, 2003 as a basic GSM phone with a built-in flashlight. It became the best selling mobile handset ever, at over 250 million units.

Basic GSM handset for cost-conscious and developing markets with monophonic ringtones and a flashlight. It stayed in production until 2010.
