XBAND (1994)

Modem for Genesis and later SNES from Catapult Entertainment that matched players over the phone line for online play. The Genesis version launched November 17, 1994 and service ended April 30, 1997.

2,400 bps modem that matched players for online Genesis and SNES games. It cost $19.99 and ended service April 30, 1997.
