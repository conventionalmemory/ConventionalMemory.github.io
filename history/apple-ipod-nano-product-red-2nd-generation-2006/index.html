Apple iPod nano (PRODUCT)RED (2nd generation) (2006)

A red special-edition nano that donated part of sales to the Global Fund.

The (PRODUCT)RED nano, from October 13, 2006 for 4 GB and November 3, 2006 for 8 GB, was Apple's first (PRODUCT)RED device. Part of sales supported the Global Fund. Red nanos appeared in later generations too.
