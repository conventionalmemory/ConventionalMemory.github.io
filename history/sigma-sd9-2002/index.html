Sigma SD9 (2002)

First production camera with the Foveon X3 sensor.

Launched Feb 18, 2002 at PMA, it captured full colour at every pixel and gave 2268x1512x3 output. It was Sigma's first digital camera and used CompactFlash/MicroDrive.
