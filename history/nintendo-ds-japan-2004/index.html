Nintendo DS (Japan) (2004)

Nintendo's dual-screen handheld launched in Japan on December 2, 2004, ahead of its North American release. The lower screen was a touchscreen and the system played Game Boy Advance cartridges.

Handheld with an ARM946E-S and an ARM7TDMI processor, 4 MB of RAM and two 3 inch 256x192 screens. It had a Game Card slot and a Game Boy Advance cartridge slot.
