Final Fantasy XI (2002)

Square's first MMO Final Fantasy, released on PS2 in Japan on May 16, 2002. The Windows version followed on November 7, 2002. It required a monthly subscription.

Square's first massively multiplayer Final Fantasy, released in Japan in May 2002 for PC and PS2.
