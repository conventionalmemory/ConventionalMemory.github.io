Nokia 3310 (2000)

Announced in September 2000 and sold from late 2000, it replaced the Nokia 3210. A Series 20 GSM phone with a monochrome 84x48 display, it sold about 126 million units.

Candy bar GSM phone that became known for durability, with a 1.5 inch monochrome LCD and the Snake game. About 126 million were sold before it was discontinued in 2005.
