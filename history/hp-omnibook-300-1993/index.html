HP OmniBook 300 (1993)

HP's first OmniBook, with a flash memory disk and a pop-out mouse. Priced by storage option.

Weighed 2.9 lb and offered no-storage, 10 MB flash or 40 MB hard-drive versions, with applications stored in ROM.
