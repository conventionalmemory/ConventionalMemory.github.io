ZX Spectrum +3 (1987)

Amstrad's Spectrum with a built-in 3-inch floppy disk drive. The launch price of 249 pounds was later cut to 199 pounds.

The ZX Spectrum +3 was released in 1987 with an integrated 3-inch disk drive in place of the +2's cassette deck.
