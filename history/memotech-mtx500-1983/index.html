Memotech MTX500 (1983)

British Z80A home computer from Memotech with 32 KB of RAM and 16 KB of video RAM. The MTX512 with 64 KB was 315 pounds.

British Z80A home computer sold as the MTX500 (32 KB) and MTX512 (64 KB).
