Pitfall II: Lost Caverns (1984)

Platform game by David Crane for the Atari 2600, published by Activision. It was later ported to many home computers and consoles in 1984.

Pitfall II was released on the Atari 2600 on February 17, 1984 and ported to the Atari 5200, ColecoVision, Commodore 64, IBM PCjr and Apple II.
