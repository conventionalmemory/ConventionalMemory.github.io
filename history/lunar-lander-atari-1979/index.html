Lunar Lander (Atari) (1979)

Atari's first vector game, released in August 1979. It is the first game with multiple perspectives, zooming as the lander nears the surface.

Atari built a vector graphics engine after Cinematronics' Space Wars, and Wendi Allen proposed a Lunar Lander game.
