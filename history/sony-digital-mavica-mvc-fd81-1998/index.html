Sony Digital Mavica MVC-FD81 (1998)

First Mavica to capture video, with 1024x768 stills.

The FD81 was described as the first Mavica able to record video clips, in two resolutions. Stills were 1024x768 JPEGs on the floppy. It added motion capture to the floppy-disk concept.
