Commodore 16 (1984)

Low-end model of the Commodore 264 series with 16 KB of RAM and Commodore BASIC 3.5. It was priced to replace the VIC-20.

The Commodore 16 was released in 1984 at US$99 as the entry-level machine of the 264 series.
