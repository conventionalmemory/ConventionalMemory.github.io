Atari Falcon030 (1992)

The last personal computer from Atari Corporation, with a 16 MHz 68030 and a Motorola 56001 DSP. It sold in small numbers and was dropped in late 1993 for the Jaguar.

Last personal computer from Atari, with a 68030 and a Motorola 56001 DSP, a VIDEL video controller and 262,144 colors. It sold in small numbers.
