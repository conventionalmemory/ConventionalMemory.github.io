Sharp X68000 (1987)

Japanese home computer with a 10 MHz Motorola 68000 and 1 MB of RAM. It was known for close arcade conversions.

The X68000 was a Japanese-only home computer built around a 10 MHz Motorola 68000 and 1 MB of RAM, with no hard drive in the first model. It was revealed in October 1986 and released in 1987, and was known for close arcade conversions.
