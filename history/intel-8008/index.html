Intel 8008 (1972)

Intel's first 8-bit microprocessor, introduced in April 1972 at $120. It needed roughly 30 support chips to interface memory.

Launched in April 1972 at $120. Single 8-bit bus shared for several functions, which made memory interfacing cumbersome.
