Tandy DeskMate (1984)

Menu-driven integrated application suite and shell from Tandy, first released in 1984 and bundled with the Tandy 1000. Later versions ran on many Tandy and PC systems.

DeskMate bundled a word processor, spreadsheet and other applications behind one graphical-text shell on Tandy computers.
