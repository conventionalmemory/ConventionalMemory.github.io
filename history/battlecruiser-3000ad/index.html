Battlecruiser 3000AD (1996)

An ambitious, long-delayed space sim with a cult following.

Take-Two's first big title (14.2% of its FY1996 revenue) and subject of a Usenet flame war of over 70,000 posts; GameSpot called it among the most bug-ridden releases ever.
