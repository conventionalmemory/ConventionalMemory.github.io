Voodoo Rush (1997)

3dfx's integrated 2D/3D board, the first step toward Voodoo Banshee.

Cards from Hercules and Intergraph combined an Alliance or Macronix 2D chip with Voodoo Graphics. Poor sales and compatibility got it discontinued within a year.
