Creative Nomad Jukebox (2000)

An early hard-drive MP3 player from Creative that beat the iPod to market by about a year.

Creative's Nomad Jukebox shipped in the U.S. in September 2000 and used a laptop hard drive. It was about the size of a portable CD player. By January 2001 Creative reported 100,000 units sold. Later models used Microdrives.
