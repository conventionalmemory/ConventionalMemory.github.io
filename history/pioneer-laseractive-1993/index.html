Pioneer LaserActive (1993)

A modular LaserDisc player that played Sega and NEC games through PAC add-on modules.

Launched in Japan on August 20, 1993 at 89,800 yen and in North America on September 13 at $970. Optional Mega-LD and LD-ROM2 PACs added Genesis/Sega CD and PC Engine play. Only about 10,000 sold, with 31 titles released, and it was dropped in 1996.
