ZX Spectrum +2 (1986)

First Spectrum released after Amstrad bought Sinclair's computer business. It is a 128 KB model with a built-in cassette recorder.

The ZX Spectrum +2 appeared in 1986 shortly after Amstrad's acquisition of Sinclair's computer business and sold at 149 pounds.
