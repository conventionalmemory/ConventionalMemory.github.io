Mac mini (2005)

Apple's low-cost Mac introduced at Macworld, sold without display, keyboard or mouse.

Announced January 2005 with a PowerPC G4 CPU; the base price is recalled as $499, but a fetched Wikipedia summary gave $799, so verify.
