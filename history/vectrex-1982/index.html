Vectrex (1982)

The only home console with its own vector display, with the built-in game Mine Storm.

Milton Bradley bought GCE and lost $31.6 million when the 1983 crash hit; it was discontinued in February 1984. It offered the first 3D console peripheral, the 3D Imager, and a light pen.
