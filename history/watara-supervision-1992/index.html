Watara Supervision (1992)

A $49.95 budget Game Boy rival, also sold as the QuickShot Supervision in the UK.

Launched before Christmas 1992 with eighteen games and a tiltable 160x160 four-grey screen, plus an optional TV link that gave four-color output. Over 60 games came out, mostly from Asian studios. The poor screen and weak library kept it behind the Game Boy despite the price.
