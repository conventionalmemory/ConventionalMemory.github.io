eMac (2002)

Apple's education-oriented all-in-one with a 17 inch flat CRT, introduced on April 29, 2002 and later sold to consumers.

PowerPC G4 at 700 MHz with 128 MB RAM and a 17 inch flat CRT display at 1280x960.
