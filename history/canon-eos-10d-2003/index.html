Canon EOS 10D (2003)

6.3 MP DSLR, first Canon DSLR with the DIGIC processor.

Announced Feb 27, 2003 at $1,999, the 10D had a magnesium-alloy body and 7-point autofocus. It was the first Canon DSLR with DIGIC and FAT32 card support, and added PictBridge via a firmware update. It shared its sensor with the 300D.
