Intellivoice (1982)

Speech synthesis module for the Mattel Intellivision using the General Instrument SP0256. Its launch games included Space Spartans, Bomb Squad and B-17 Bomber.

Voice synthesis add-on for the Intellivision, debuting at about $100.
