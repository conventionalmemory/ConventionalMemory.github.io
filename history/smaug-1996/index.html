SMAUG (1996)

Thoric's heavily extended Merc-based codebase from Realms of Despair, and the base of many MUDs, including Star Wars Reality.

SMAUG came out of Realms of Despair and added many features, notably in-game area building. Many MUDs run on it, and Star Wars Reality was built from an early SMAUG codebase.
