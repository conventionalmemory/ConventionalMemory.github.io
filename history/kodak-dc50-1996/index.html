Kodak DC50 (1996)

Zoom-lens Kodak digital camera with PCMCIA card storage.

The DC50 added a 3x zoom lens and PCMCIA card storage to the DC series, aimed at business users. It connected by serial and could be used with Mac and PC. It was a high point for Kodak's early consumer-prosumer cameras.
