Sega Saturn (Japan) (1994)

The Japanese launch of Sega's 32-bit console, ahead of Sony's PlayStation by about a week.

Launched November 22, 1994 at 44,800 yen with dual SH-2 CPUs. It sold 5.75 million in Japan of 9.26 million worldwide. Virtua Fighter 2 was the best seller, and exclusives included Nights into Dreams and Panzer Dragoon.
