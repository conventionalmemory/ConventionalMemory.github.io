Kodak DC4800 (2000)

3.1 MP Kodak zoom camera with USB and CompactFlash.

The DC4800 reached 3.1 MP with a 3x zoom lens and saved to CompactFlash. It connected over USB with Kodak software and was one of the company's last pre-EasyShare cameras. It sits near the DC series' end of line.
