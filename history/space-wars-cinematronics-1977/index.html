Space Wars (Cinematronics) (1977)

Cinematronics' vector Space Wars, released in November 1977, was an arcade version of Spacewar! and the highest-earning US arcade game of 1978.

Sold 10,000 cabinets in 1978 and set the hardware base for Star Castle and Tail Gunner.
