Commodore 1351 Mouse (1986)

Commodore's official proportional mouse for the C64 and C128.

It used the SID chip's analog-to-digital converters to read movement and could emulate a joystick if you held the right button at power-up. It shipped with a manual, demos and a GEOS upgrade and was praised for its build.
