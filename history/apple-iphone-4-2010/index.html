Apple iPhone 4 (2010)

First iPhone with the 960x640 Retina display and the Apple A4 chip. It added FaceTime video calling and shipped with iOS 4.

Fourth iPhone, available June 24, 2010, with a 326 ppi Retina display, a stainless steel frame and the Apple A4 SoC.
