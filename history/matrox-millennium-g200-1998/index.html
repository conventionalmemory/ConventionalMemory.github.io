Matrox Millennium G200 (1998)

Matrox's first fully AGP graphics chip, with 32-bit color and dual 64-bit buses. It shipped in 1998 and was the first Matrox chip to need a heatsink.

A 128-bit core with DualBus architecture. Most boards shipped with 8 MB, expandable to 16 MB with an add-on module.
