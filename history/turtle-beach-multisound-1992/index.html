Turtle Beach MultiSound (1992)

A premium PC sound card with an E-mu synth and a DSP, aimed at musicians rather than gamers.

Used high-quality converters, an E-mu wavetable synthesizer and an onboard DSP. It sold on audio quality and Windows 3.1 support, competing with Gravis and Creative on price and fidelity.
