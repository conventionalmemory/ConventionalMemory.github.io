Home Pong (1975)

Atari's single-game home version of Pong, sold exclusively through Sears under the Tele-Games name in 1975.

Sold from late October to mid November 1975, Home Pong used LSI chip technology to shrink the arcade game to a living-room unit. It sparked a wave of Pong clones and helped establish Atari as a consumer brand.
