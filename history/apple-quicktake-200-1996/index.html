Apple QuickTake 200 (1996)

Fujifilm-built QuickTake with SmartMedia and a colour LCD.

The last QuickTake used a Fujifilm design, a 1.8-inch LCD and removable 2 MB SmartMedia cards holding about 20 images. It connected by serial and could output NTSC video. It was Mac-oriented and was discontinued in 1997 when Steve Jobs cut non-computer products.
