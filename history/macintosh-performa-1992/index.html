Macintosh Performa (1992)

Apple's consumer Macintosh brand, sold from 1992 to 1997 through retailers such as Good Guys, Circuit City and Sears. The first models were the Performa 200, 400 and 600, and Apple eventually shipped 64 Performa models.

Apple's retail-channel Mac brand from 1992 to 1997, first the Performa 200, 400 and 600. Sold with bundled software and monitors.
