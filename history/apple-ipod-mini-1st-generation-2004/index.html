Apple iPod mini (1st generation) (2004)

A compact 4 GB iPod in five anodized aluminum colors with a Click Wheel.

The iPod mini used a Microdrive and introduced the Click Wheel. It came in five colors and was Apple's mid-size iPod before the nano. Its 30-pin dock connector supported USB or FireWire. Collectors prize the gold model, which was discontinued with the 2nd generation.
