Nikon D1 (1999)

Nikon's first in-house pro DSLR, 2.7 MP.

The Nikon D1 was Nikon's first in-house professional DSLR, released June 15, 1999 at US$5,500 for the body. It had a 2.7 megapixel sensor and shot 4.5 frames per second.
