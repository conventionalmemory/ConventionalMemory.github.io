Tandy Color Computer 3 (1986)

Third generation of the TRS-80 Color Computer, with 128 KB of RAM expandable to 512 KB. It was announced at the Waldorf-Astoria in New York.

The Color Computer 3 was released on July 30, 1986 and sold at Radio Shack stores and Tandy Computer Centers for 219.95 dollars.
