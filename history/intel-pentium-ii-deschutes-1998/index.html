Intel Pentium II (Deschutes) (1998)

The 250 nm Pentium II core, which raised speeds to 266 through 450 MHz. It succeeded the 233 to 300 MHz Klamath parts.

The Deschutes core used a 250 nm process and ran at 266 to 450 MHz.
