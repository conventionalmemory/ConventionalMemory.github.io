Nintendo Switch (OLED model) (2021)

A Switch revision with a 7 inch OLED screen, 64 GB storage and a better kickstand.

Released October 8, 2021 at $349.99, it keeps the original's dock and Joy-Con design, adding enhanced audio. Nintendo's Switch family had passed Nintendo's longest-running console record by July 2024.
