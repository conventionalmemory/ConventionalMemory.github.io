Samsung Galaxy S (2010)

First phone in Samsung's Galaxy S line, released in June 2010 with a 4 inch Super AMOLED display and a 1 GHz Hummingbird CPU.

Launched with Android 2.1 Eclair and became a best-selling Android phone.
