Nokia 9000 Communicator (1996)

Nokia's first Communicator, with a 640 x 200 monochrome display. It launched in the UK at 1,000 pounds.

A phone with a PDA side running PEN/GEOS 3.0 and a 24 MHz i386 CPU.
