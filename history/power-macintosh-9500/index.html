Power Macintosh 9500 (1995)

First Apple computer with PCI slots, with a PowerPC 604 CPU and six PCI slots. Released June 19, 1995 with 16 MB of RAM and a 4x CD-ROM drive.

First Apple computer with PCI slots and 168-pin DIMMs, with a PowerPC 604. Shipped with 16 MB of RAM, a 1 GB drive and 4x CD-ROM.
