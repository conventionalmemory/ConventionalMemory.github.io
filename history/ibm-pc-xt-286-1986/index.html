IBM PC XT 286 (1986)

IBM PC XT-style machine with an 80286 CPU, 640 KB of RAM, a 1.2 MB AT-style diskette drive and a 20 MB hard disk.

The IBM XT 286 (Model 5162) was introduced in 1986 and discontinued in late 1987 after the PS/2 launch.
