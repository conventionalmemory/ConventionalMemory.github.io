Doom Eternal (2020)

Sequel to Doom (2016) with Glory Kills and Battlemode.

Doom Eternal sold about 3 million digital copies by March 2020, the franchise's highest opening weekend. The Ancient Gods DLC arrived October 20, 2020 and March 18, 2021. Metacritic: PC 88, PS4 87, Xbox One 88.
