WordPerfect 2.20 (IBM PC) (1982)

First WordPerfect for the IBM PC, released the day after Thanksgiving 1982 by Satellite Software International. It continued the version numbering of the Data General program.

First IBM PC release of WordPerfect, sold as version 2.20.
