PlayStation Portable Slim and Lite (PSP-2000) (2007)

A slimmer, lighter PSP with 64 MB RAM and USB charging.

Released first in Hong Kong on August 30, 2007, then Europe September 5 and North America September 6. It was 0.73 inches thick versus 0.91 and weighed 6.66 oz versus 9.87 oz. PSP lifetime sales reached 76.4 million by March 2012.
