Fujifilm FinePix X100 (2011)

Retro fixed-lens camera with a hybrid viewfinder.

Announced September 2010 and released February 2011 at $1,199, it had a 12 MP APS-C CMOS sensor and a hybrid optical/electronic viewfinder. It won a TIPA award and revived Fujifilm's premium compacts.
