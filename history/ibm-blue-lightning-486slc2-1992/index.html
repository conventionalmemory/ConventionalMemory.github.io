IBM Blue Lightning 486SLC2 (1992)

A 486-class chip that dropped into 386SX and DX boards.

IBM's 486SLC2 ('Blue Lightning') was sold as an upgrade for 386 owners, with clock-doubled speed and 16 KB cache. Quality varied by board.
