Colossal Cave Adventure (1976)

William Crowther developed the first text adventure on a PDP-10 at BBN in 1975 to 1976. Don Woods expanded it in 1977.

Original had about 66 rooms. Woods's 1977 expansion reached 140 locations and added a scoring system.
