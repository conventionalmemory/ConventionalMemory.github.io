AmigaOS 1.0 (1985)

The Amiga's multitasking operating system, shipped with Kickstart and Workbench 1.0 on the Amiga 1000.

AmigaOS 1.0 shipped with the Amiga 1000 and offered preemptive multitasking and a graphical Workbench desktop.
