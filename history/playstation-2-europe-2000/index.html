PlayStation 2 (Europe) (2000)

The PlayStation 2 went on sale in Europe on November 24, 2000 at 299 pounds, with limited supply. Pre-orders were capped at about 80,000 units.

European launch of the PlayStation 2 with a 294.912 MHz Emotion Engine and 32 MB of RDRAM.
