Duke Nukem (1991)

Apogee.

Duke Nukem is a single-player 2D platform game with 320x200 16-color EGA graphics, developed and published by Apogee for MS-DOS. Producer Scott Miller, programmer Todd Replogle and designers Replogle, Miller and Allen H. Blum III made it; George Broussard, Jim Norwood and Blum did the art. John Carmack helped with low-level assembly code. Scott Miller estimated 60,000-70,000 copies sold. Flux magazine ranked it 39th on its 1995 Top 100 list, calling it the best PC platform game ever created.
