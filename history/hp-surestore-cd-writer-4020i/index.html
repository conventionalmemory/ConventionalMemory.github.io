HP SureStore CD-Writer 4020i (1995)

The first CD recorder to cost under $1,000.

In September 1995 HP's 4020i, made by Philips, sold for $995. By 1992 recorders cost $10,000 to $12,000. Home CD-R backup and audio mastering became realistic.
