TV Typewriter (1973)

Don Lancaster's TV Typewriter, a do-it-yourself video terminal, was on the cover of Radio-Electronics in September 1973. Readers could order a 16-page $2 construction package; about 10,000 did, more than could be filled.

Published September 1973 in Radio-Electronics. A 16-page construction package was offered for $2.
