Hartung Game Master (1990)

A budget Game Boy competitor sold in Europe, listed among the first challengers to Nintendo's handheld.

Little is documented beyond its appearance in lists of 1990 handhelds; it competed with a long tail of cheap Game Boy alternatives on price.
