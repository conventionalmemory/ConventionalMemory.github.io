Sol-20 (1976)

Processor Technology's Sol-20 terminal computer, designed by Lee Felsenstein, appeared on the July 1976 cover of Popular Electronics. It sold at $995 as a kit and $1,495 assembled.

Announced in mid-1976 and shipped in late 1976. The Sol-20 was the dominant model of the Sol line.
