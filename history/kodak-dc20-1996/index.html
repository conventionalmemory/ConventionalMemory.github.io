Kodak DC20 (1996)

Entry-level Kodak digital camera with a serial link to Mac and Windows.

An inexpensive point-and-shoot using Kodak's early consumer design with a fixed lens and small internal memory. Images moved to a PC over a serial cable with bundled software. It was Kodak's budget option under the DC40.
