Fujifilm FinePix S1 Pro (2000)

3.1 MP Super CCD DSLR on a Nikon F60 body.

Released January 2000, the S1 Pro used a diagonal-photodiode Super CCD that Fujifilm said gave 6.2 MP output. It had a Nikon F mount and ISO 320-1600. It was succeeded by the S2 Pro.
