WonderSwan (1999)

Gunpei Yokoi's last project, a cheap long-battery handheld.

Launched March 4, 1999 at 4,800 yen with a 16-bit NEC V30 and a 224x144 screen, 40 hours per AA battery. 3.5 million sold across the family; Japan only.
