Lighthouse: The Dark Being (1996)

Sierra's Myst-like adventure on the Oregon coast.

The player investigates a lighthouse and pursues a supernatural being into a parallel world. Aimed explicitly at the Myst audience, it stressed character interaction and story. Reviews were mediocre: good visuals, weak puzzles.
