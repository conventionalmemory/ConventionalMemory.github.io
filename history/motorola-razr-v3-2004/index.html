Motorola Razr V3 (2004)

Announced on July 27, 2004 and first sold in Asia in September, then in the US on November 16, 2004. Its 13 mm aluminum clamshell body made it the best selling flip phone of the decade.

Thin aluminum flip phone with a 2.2 inch 176x220 TFT display and a VGA camera. Sales are cited at about 130 million units.
