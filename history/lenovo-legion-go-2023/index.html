Lenovo Legion Go (2023)

A Windows handheld gaming PC with detachable controllers and an 8.8 inch QHD screen.

Released October 31, 2023 at $599.99 to $749.99. It used AMD Ryzen Z1 or Z1 Extreme with 16 GB RAM. The Legion Go 2 arrived October 2025.
