Magnavox Odyssey 300 (1976)

The first Odyssey to use a single-chip design (the AY-3-8500), with digital on-screen scoring.

Released in October 1976 at $69, it competed with Coleco's Telstar and offered Tennis, Hockey and Smash with three difficulty levels and a single control dial. The Odyssey 400 and 500 followed later in 1976, and Odyssey 2000, 3000 and 4000 in 1977.
