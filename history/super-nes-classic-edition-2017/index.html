Super NES Classic Edition (2017)

A mini Super NES with 21 games, including the first official release of the cancelled Star Fox 2.

Released September 29, 2017 at $79.99 in North America and Europe, and October 5 in Japan as the Nintendo Classic Mini: Super Famicom. It reused the NES Classic's Allwinner R16 guts. It sold over 5 million units by March 2018. Star Fox 2 had been cancelled in 1996 and was unlocked by beating the first level of Star Fox.
