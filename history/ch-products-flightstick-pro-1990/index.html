CH Products Flightstick Pro (1990)

Four-button stick with a throttle wheel and hat switch, long a PC sim staple.

The Flightstick Pro offered a hat switch and a throttle wheel on the base. Together with Virtual Pilot and the Pro Pedals, CH built a full stick, yoke and rudder lineup that stayed on sale for decades.
