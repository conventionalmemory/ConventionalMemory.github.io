iBook G4 (2003)

Apple's iBook line moved to the PowerPC G4 on October 22, 2003 and ran until MacBooks replaced it in 2006.

12.1 or 14.1 inch laptop with a 800 MHz to 1 GHz G4, 256 MB RAM and a slot-loading optical drive.
