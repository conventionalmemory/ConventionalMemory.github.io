Doom RPG (2005)

Mobile turn-based first-person RPG directed and programmed by John Carmack. Released September 19, 2005 on Java ME and BREW handsets.

Doom RPG launched September 19, 2005 on Java and BREW handsets. Gameplay is turn-based. GameSpot scored it 8.6/10, and it sold over 1 million copies. Its engine was reused for Orcs & Elves, Wolfenstein RPG and Doom II RPG.
