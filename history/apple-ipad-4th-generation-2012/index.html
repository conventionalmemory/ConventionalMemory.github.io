Apple iPad (4th generation) (2012)

Fourth iPad, with the Apple A6X chip and a Lightning connector in place of the 30-pin dock connector. It replaced the third generation only about seven months after that model.

Released November 2, 2012 in 35 countries, with a rollout through December 2012.
