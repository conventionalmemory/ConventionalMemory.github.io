Logitech MouseMan (1990)

A Logitech mouse shaped to the hand, one of the first ergonomic PC mice.

The MouseMan used a sculpted right-hand shape rather than the rectangles of the 1980s, and its design lineage runs through the later MouseMan Wheel and MX line.
