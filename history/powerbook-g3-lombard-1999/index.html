PowerBook G3 (Lombard) (1999)

The bronze-keyboard PowerBook G3 with a 14.1 inch display, 333 or 400 MHz G3 and the line's first USB ports.

A PowerBook G3 with a 14.1 inch 1024 x 768 screen and 64 MB of PC100 SDRAM standard.
