Apple iPod Hi-Fi (2006)

A speaker system with a 30-pin dock for iPods and a line input.

Apple's only branded speaker system for iPods, the Hi-Fi had a dock and an auxiliary input. It could run on batteries. It was criticized for its high price and lack of FM radio. Apple discontinued it September 5, 2007.
