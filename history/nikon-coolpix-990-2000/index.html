Nikon Coolpix 990 (2000)

3.1 MP swivel Coolpix with a 1/1.8-inch CCD.

The 990 raised resolution to 2048x1536 on a 1/1.8-inch CCD and saved to CompactFlash. It was a popular enthusiast camera of 2000, using USB for PC transfer.
