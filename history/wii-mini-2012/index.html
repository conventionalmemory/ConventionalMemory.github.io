Wii Mini (2012)

Budget Wii without online features, SD slot or GameCube support. It launched in Canada on December 7, 2012 (CA$99.99) and reached the US on November 17, 2013.

Wii Mini launched in Canada in December 2012 as a stripped-down redesign.
