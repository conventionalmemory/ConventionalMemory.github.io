Intel 80386SX (1988)

A cost-reduced 386 with a 16-bit external bus, used in the first 386SX PC, the Compaq Deskpro 386S of mid-1988.

It gave budget PCs 386 software compatibility and later underpinned many early Windows 3.x machines.
