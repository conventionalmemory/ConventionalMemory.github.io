NEC PC Engine Duo (1991)

The PC Engine with its CD-ROM drive built in, combining HuCard and CD-ROM2 support in one box.

Released in Japan on September 21, 1991 at 59,800 yen. It later got the cheaper Duo-R (March 1993) and Duo-RX (June 1994). Bundles included Ys Book I & II on CD. The North American version was the TurboDuo.
