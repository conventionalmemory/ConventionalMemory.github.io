Sound Blaster 1.5 (1990)

A cost-reduced Sound Blaster revision that dropped the CMS chips, which launched as an empty socket.

Model CT1320C kept the Yamaha YM3812 OPL2 FM chip and 8-bit DAC but left the Game Blaster chip sockets unpopulated. It was the Sound Blaster most DOS-era buyers got before the Pro.
