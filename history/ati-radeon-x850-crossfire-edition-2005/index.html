ATI Radeon X850 CrossFire Edition (2005)

ATI's CrossFire master card, the launch hardware for its dual-GPU answer to SLI.

You bought a Master card and paired it with a normal card of the same series. X800, X850, X1800 and X1900 had CrossFire Edition versions.
