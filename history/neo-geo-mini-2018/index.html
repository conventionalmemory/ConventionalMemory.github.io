Neo Geo Mini (2018)

Handheld-sized Neo Geo arcade cabinet replica with a 3.5 inch LCD and HDMI out. 40 games in Japan, with a different 40-game lineup abroad from October 2018.

Wikipedia lists the Neo Geo Mini as a 2018 SNK release, sold in multiple editions including Christmas and Samurai Shodown versions. It followed the Neo Geo X and was part of SNK's push into mini hardware that also yielded the Neo Geo Arcade Stick Pro (2019) and Neo Geo MVSX (2020).
