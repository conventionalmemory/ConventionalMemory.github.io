Excitebike (1984)

Motocross racing game by Nintendo R&D4, released on the Famicom on November 30, 1984. Its scrolling engine was later reused for Super Mario Bros.

Excitebike was directed by Shigeru Miyamoto and released for the NES in North America on October 18, 1985.
