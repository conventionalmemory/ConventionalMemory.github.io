ATI 3D Rage (1996)

ATI's first 3D chip, a Mach64 2D core with 3D and MPEG-1 added.

Shipped first in OEM PCs like the IBM Aptiva. It was the first step of the Rage line that led to Rage Pro and Rage 128.
