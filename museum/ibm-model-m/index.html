IBM Model M

The Model M is IBM's buckling-spring keyboard, introduced in 1985 as the IBM Enhanced Keyboard. The original 1391401 used a 101-key layout, with either an AT or a PS/2 connector, and most units until about 1993 had a detachable coiled cable.
