Toshiba Libretto 110CT

A pocket-sized Toshiba subnotebook from the Libretto line: a Pentium MMX 233 with a 7.1-inch 800 x 480 screen, small enough for a coat pocket yet able to run full Windows 95 or 98. Toshiba announced it in Europe in November 1998.
